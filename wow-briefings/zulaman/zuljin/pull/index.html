TL;DR

  • Five phases, one per animal spirit, switching at 80/60/40/20%.

  • Bear: Creeping Paralysis on the raid — dispel fast!
  • Eagle: Energy Storm — casting hurts you; Feather Vortexes.
  • Lynx: Claw Rage / Lynx Rush on random players.
  • Dragonhawk: Flame Whirl, Pillar of Fire — move.

Holy Priest

  • Eagle: each heal costs you HP — Renew/PoM

Shadow Priest

  • Help dispel Creeping Paralysis
