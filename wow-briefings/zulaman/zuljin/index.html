Raid leadPull view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Five phases, one per animal spirit, switching at 80/60/40/20%.
  • Troll: Grievous Throw — bleeds until healed to full.
  • Bear: Creeping Paralysis on the raid — dispel fast!
  • Eagle: Energy Storm — casting hurts you; Feather Vortexes.
  • Lynx: Claw Rage / Lynx Rush on random players.
  • Dragonhawk: Flame Whirl, Pillar of Fire — move.
Affliction Warlock
  • Eagle phase: DoTs = fewer casts = less Energy Storm damage.
  • Dragonhawk: move out of pillars, keep DoTs up.
Holy Priest
  • Creeping Paralysis: dispel fast (Mass Dispel works).
  • Grievous Throw: top that player fully.
  • Eagle: every spell hurts — efficient HoTs.
Shadow Priest
  • Eagle phase: DoTs instead of Mind Flay spam.
  • Help dispel Creeping Paralysis.

Strategy

Phases switch at 80/60/40/20%. Heal Grievous Throw to full, mass-dispel Creeping Paralysis, avoid Feather Vortexes, dodge fire in the last phase.
