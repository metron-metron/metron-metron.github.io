Zul'Aman

Raid Lead

Composition

  • 2 tanks (Nalorakk swap; Halazzi lynx split), 3 healers, 5 DPS.
  • At least one dispeller for magic (Halazzi Flame Shock, Zul'jin Creeping Paralysis) — priests/paladins.

Timer

  • Gong starts the timer. Each animal boss in time adds time.
  • Common order: Nalorakk → Akil'zon → Jan'alai → Halazzi; skip unnecessary trash.
  • Amani'shi Scouts run to drums and call reinforcements — assign stuns/kills before they reach them.

Trash

  • Mark CC on every pull; troll casters need interrupts.

Bosses

  • 1. Akil'zon
  • 2. Nalorakk
  • 3. Jan'alai
  • 4. Halazzi
  • 5. Hex Lord Malacrass
  • 6. Zul'jin
