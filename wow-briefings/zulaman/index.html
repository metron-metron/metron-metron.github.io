Overview

10-man troll raid. Four animal bosses (Akil'zon, Nalorakk, Jan'alai, Halazzi), then Hex Lord Malacrass and Zul'jin. Timed event: each animal boss killed in time frees a prisoner and gives a bonus chest. All four in time → Amani War Bear rewards.

Trash

  • Troll packs hit hard — CC first. Warlocks: Fear and Curse of Tongues on casters; Seed of Corruption on grouped trash once it's tanked.
  • Banish has few targets here.

Consumables

Flask, food, potions. Nothing special.
