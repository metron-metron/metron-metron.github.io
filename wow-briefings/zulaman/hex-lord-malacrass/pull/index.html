Hex Lord Malacrass

TL;DR

  • Comes with four of eight possible adds — kill order called by the RL.
  • Spirit Bolts: channeled shadow damage on the whole raid.
  • Siphon Soul: drains a random player and gains that class's abilities for a while.
  • Drain Power: stacking damage buff on him.

Affliction Warlock

  • If he copies a warlock: Rain of Fire, curses — move/heal
  • DoTs on adds per kill order

Holy Priest

  • Spirit Bolts = group heals
  • If he copies a priest he heals — call it

Shadow Priest

  • DoTs on adds; VT during Spirit Bolts helps the raid
