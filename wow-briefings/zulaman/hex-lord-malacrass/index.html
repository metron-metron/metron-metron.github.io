Hex Lord Malacrass

Raid leadPull view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Comes with four of eight possible adds — kill order called by the RL.
  • Spirit Bolts: channeled shadow damage on the whole raid.
  • Siphon Soul: drains a random player and gains that class's abilities for a while.
  • Drain Power: stacking damage buff on him.
Affliction Warlock
  • Kill order on adds; DoTs on the longer-lived ones.
  • Copied warlock abilities: watch for Rain of Fire and curses.
Holy Priest
  • Spirit Bolts = PoH/CoH.
  • If he steals a priest, he heals himself — call it out.
Shadow Priest
  • DoTs on adds; VT on Malacrass for the raid's mana.

Strategy

Kill adds first, then the boss. Deal with whatever class set he steals. Healers prepare for Spirit Bolts.

Abilities

  • Spirit Bolts, Siphon Soul, Drain Power, plus stolen class abilities.
