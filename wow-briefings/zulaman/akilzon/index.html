TL;DR

  • Spread for Static Disruption (nature vulnerability on the target and anyone near them).
  • Electrical Storm: a random player is lifted. Everyone stacks directly under them — outside the eye is lethal.
  • Soaring Eagles spawn — kill them.
Affliction Warlock
  • Spread normally; stack under the lifted player on storms.
  • Full DoTs; eagles die to AoE.
Holy Priest
  • Static Disruption victims take extra nature damage — heal them first.
  • Electrical Storm: PoH/CoH on the stacked raid.
Shadow Priest
  • Move under the storm target first, DPS second.

Strategy

Raid spreads in a circle. When Electrical Storm lifts someone, everyone moves under them. Heal up, spread again, repeat.

Abilities
