Raid Lead

Setup

  • 1 tank on the boss, 1 for hatchlings; AoE DPS (warlock Seed, mage) for hatchlings.

Positioning

  • Boss in the middle of the platform.

Egg control

  • Amani'shi Hatchers run to the egg platforms on both sides and hatch eggs.
  • Let most eggs hatch and AoE them before 35%, so few are left for the mass hatch.
  • Kill hatchers if too many hatchlings are up at once.

Wipe causes

  • Too many eggs left at 35%; enrage at 10 min.
