Jan'alai

Raid leadPull view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Flame Breath — fire line; don't stand in it.
  • Fire Bombs: the raid is pulled together and surrounded — run out through the safe gap.
  • Amani Dragonhawk Hatchlings hatch from eggs — AoE them.
  • At 35%: all remaining eggs hatch.
  • 10-minute enrage.
Affliction Warlock
  • Seed of Corruption on grouped hatchlings.
  • Single target on Jan'alai otherwise.
Holy Priest
  • Fire Bomb patterns: heal anyone clipped.
  • Hatchling waves: the AoE tank takes damage — CoH/PoH.
Shadow Priest
  • DoTs on Jan'alai; Shadow has no real AoE — leave hatchlings to AoE classes unless called.

Strategy

Stay in the middle with the raid. Dodge Fire Bombs, AoE hatchlings, DPS the boss.

Abilities

  • Flame Breath, Fire Bombs, hatchlings, mass hatch at 35%.
