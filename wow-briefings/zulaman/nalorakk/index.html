TL;DR

  • Swaps between troll and bear form.
  • Troll form: Surge charges the furthest player — one soaker stands far away; don't be further than them.
  • Bear form: Deafening Roar silences — no long casts right before; tank takes heavy bleeds.
Affliction Warlock
  • Full DoT rotation.
Holy Priest
  • Bear form tank: Lacerating Slash / Rend Flesh bleeds — big heals ready.
  • Deafening Roar: have HoTs and shields rolling before it.
Shadow Priest
  • Single target; Shadowfiend when silence isn't up.

Strategy

Stand at range but closer than the Surge soaker. Pre-heal before silences.

Abilities

  • Surge (furthest player), Deafening Roar (silence), bleeds on the tank in bear form.
