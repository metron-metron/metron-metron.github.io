Sunwell Plateau · Boss 6

Kil'jaeden

Raid leadPull view ⤢

TL;DR

  • P1: three Hand of the Deceiver casters channel — kill them and their Volatile Felfire Fiends.
  • KJ: Legion Lightning, Fire Bloom (spread), Flame Dart, Shield Orbs, Sinister Reflections (copies of players) — kill the Shield Orbs and your own Reflection.
  • Blue dragon orbs: players become dragons; Shield of the Blue absorbs Darkness of a Thousand Souls — be inside it.
  • Final phase: Armageddon meteors — keep moving.
Affliction Warlock
  • P1: DoTs on Hands.
  • KJ: Healthstone; kill your Sinister Reflection.
  • Movement-heavy — DoTs shine.
Holy Priest
  • Fire Bloom on several players: CoH/PoH.
  • Darkness of a Thousand Souls: inside Shield of the Blue.
Shadow Priest
  • DoTs on Hands, VT on KJ; you might be a dragon.

Strategy

Kill the Hands quickly. In KJ phases, spread for Fire Bloom, kill Shield Orbs and Reflections, get into the dragon's shield before Darkness. Last phase: keep moving out of meteors.
