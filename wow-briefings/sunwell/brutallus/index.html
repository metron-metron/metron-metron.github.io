TL;DR

  • Pure gear/DPS check with a 6-minute enrage — every cooldown and Destruction Potion.
  • Burn — random player DoT that spreads to nearby players. Spread out.
  • Meteor Slash — frontal hit on soak groups; stay out of his front unless you're soaking.
Affliction Warlock
  • Burn: move away from others immediately, keep DoTs ticking.
  • Full rotation + Destruction Potion.
Holy Priest
  • Meteor Slash soak groups take very heavy damage — CoH/PoH.
  • Burn victims: PoM + Flash Heal.
Shadow Priest
  • Full DPS + VT for healers.

Strategy

Ranged spread so Burn doesn't spread. Maximum DPS with all cooldowns.

Abilities

  • Burn, Meteor Slash, Berserk (enrage).
