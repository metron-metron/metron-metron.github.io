Sunwell Plateau · Boss 1

Kalecgos

Raid leadPull view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Fight on two planes. Outside: Kalecgos with Arcane Buffet (stacking raid damage) and Wild Magic (random effects on players).
  • Inside (Spectral Realm): Sathrovarr the Corruptor. Spectral Blast sends players in.
  • Curse of Boundless Agony spreads to nearby players inside — call it, priests and warlocks can't remove it.
  • After leaving the realm you have Spectral Exhaustion and can't re-enter for a while.
  • Keep DPS on your realm's boss.
Affliction Warlock
  • Spectral Realm: Sathrovarr is a demon — full DoTs.
  • Outside: DoTs on Kalecgos; Wild Magic can change your crit/cast costs.
Holy Priest
  • Inside: tank + team healing.
  • Outside: Arcane Buffet stacks up — PoH/CoH.
Shadow Priest
  • VT where you are; follow the realm rotation.

Strategy

Teams rotate between the realms. Inside, kill Sathrovarr and keep the good Kalec alive. Outside, DPS the dragon. Both bosses must be killed close together.

Abilities

  • Outside: Arcane Buffet, Wild Magic, Frost Breath.
  • Inside: Shadow Bolt Volley, Curse of Boundless Agony.
