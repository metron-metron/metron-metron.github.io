Sunwell Plateau · Boss 5

M'uru

Raid leadPull view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Phase 1: M'uru doesn't move. Adds come in waves: Shadowsword Berserkers and Shadowsword Fury Mages from the sides, Void Sentinels from portals, Dark Fiends that explode on contact. Kill Sentinels; Dark Fiends must die before they reach anyone.
  • Negative Energy damages the raid; avoid Darkness zones.
  • Phase 2: M'uru becomes Entropius — dodge Singularities and burn.
Affliction Warlock
  • Add teams: Seed/DoTs on Berserkers and Mages.
  • Dark Fiends: Shadowfury/kill fast if assigned.
  • P2: burn.
Holy Priest
  • Side add tanks take heavy damage; Negative Energy hits everyone.
  • P2: heal on the move.
Shadow Priest
  • DoTs on adds; VT on M'uru for the raid.

Strategy

Follow your add assignment; otherwise keep DPS on M'uru. Phase 2 is a burn with lots of movement.

Abilities

  • Negative Energy, Darkness, adds; Entropius: Singularity, Darkness.
