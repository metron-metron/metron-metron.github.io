Overview

The final TBC raid on the Isle of Quel'Danas. Six bosses: Kalecgos, Brutallus, Felmyst, the Eredar Twins, M'uru, Kil'jaeden. Very tight tuning — DPS, healing and execution checks on every fight. Original Sunwell was nerfed in steps; check which version the anniversary realm runs.

Trash

  • Trash is as dangerous as some bosses — stay out of AoE, follow CC marks.
  • Warlocks: Banish demons when asked; Felhunter Devour Magic helps on buffed mobs.
  • Long trash gauntlet before M'uru.

Consumables

Everything: flask, food, potions, runes, weapon oils, Destruction Potions. Shadow resistance helps some roles on the Twins and Kil'jaeden.
