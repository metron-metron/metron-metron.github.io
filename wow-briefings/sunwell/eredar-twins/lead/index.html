Raider view

Raid Lead

Setup

  • Warrior tank(s) on Sacrolash (Confounding Blow), warlock tank on Alythess.
  • Mages Spellsteal Pyrogenics (or priests dispel).
  • Decide kill order before the pull.

Positioning

  • Twins far apart; raid spread between; plan where Touched players go.

Calls

  • Kill target; "Conflagration — spread."

Wipe causes

  • Alythess loose on the raid (warlock tank lost threat/died).
  • Flame/Dark Touched stacks mismanaged.
