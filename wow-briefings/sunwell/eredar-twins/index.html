Sunwell Plateau · Boss 4

Eredar Twins

Raid leadPull view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Two bosses: Lady Sacrolash (shadow) and Grand Warlock Alythess (fire caster).
  • Alythess is usually tanked at range by a warlock using Searing Pain.
  • Conflagration and Shadow Nova hit random players — spread.
  • Flame Touched / Dark Touched debuffs build up — positioning handles them.
Affliction Warlock
  • If you're the Alythess tank: Searing Pain spam, Shadow Ward when needed, stay at range.
  • Otherwise: DoTs on the kill target; move for Conflagration.
Holy Priest
  • Heal your assigned tank or group.
  • Pyrogenics can be removed with offensive Dispel Magic if asked.
Shadow Priest
  • DPS the kill target; VT for mana.

Strategy

Bosses are tanked apart; raid spread in between. Kill one first (RL decides), then the other.

Abilities

  • Sacrolash: Shadow Nova, shadow images.
  • Alythess: Conflagration, Pyrogenics (self-buff, dispellable).
