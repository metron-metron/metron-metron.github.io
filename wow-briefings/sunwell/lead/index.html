Sunwell Plateau

Raid Lead

Composition

  • 3–4 tanks; Brutallus needs 3 tanks/soak groups.
  • Warlock tank for Grand Warlock Alythess (Eredar Twins).
  • Mages for Spellsteal (Twins' Pyrogenics), lots of interrupts for Sunblade/Shadowsword trash.
  • Kalecgos: two balanced teams for the Spectral Realm.

Trash

  • Sunblade packs (healer priests, mages): interrupt and CC every pull.
  • Shadowsword packs: hard hitting melee — tanks with cooldowns.
  • Pre-M'uru gauntlet: careful, slow pulls.
