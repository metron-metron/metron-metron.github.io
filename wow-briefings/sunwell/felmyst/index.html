Sunwell Plateau · Boss 3

Felmyst

Raid leadPull view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Ground phase: Gas Nova (Holy dispels it), Encapsulate (random player, damages nearby — get away from them).
  • Air phase: Demonic Vapor tracks players and spawns Unyielding Dead skeletons. Then deep breaths of Fog of Corruption — touching it mind-controls you.
  • Spread, kill skeletons, dodge breaths.
Affliction Warlock
  • Seed of Corruption on skeleton packs.
  • Ground phase: full single-target DoTs.
Holy Priest
  • Gas Nova: dispel and heal through.
  • Encapsulate: heal the trapped player and those near them.
Shadow Priest
  • DoTs; stay out of the fog.

Strategy

Spread in the ground phase. In the air phase, players targeted by Demonic Vapor move away from the raid; AoE the skeletons. Move out of the fog lane before the breath.

Abilities

  • Ground: Gas Nova, Encapsulate.
  • Air: Demonic Vapor, Unyielding Dead, Fog of Corruption.
