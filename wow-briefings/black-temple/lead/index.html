Black Temple

Raid Lead

Composition

  • 3–4 tanks (Supremus soakers, Council needs 3–4 tanking roles, Bloodboil rotation).
  • Illidan: demon phase is skipped by DPS — no warlock tank needed.
  • Rogues/warriors/shamans for interrupt rotations (Reliquary Desire, Council's Malande).
  • Shadow resistance gear for Mother Shahraz.
  • 1–2 Fire Resistance tanks for Illidan's Flames of Azzinoth.

Attunement

  • Original TBC required the Medallion of Karabor (Akama questline). Check the anniversary rules before forming a pug-heavy raid.

Trash

  • Lots of caster trash: assign interrupts and CC marks (Banish/Fear demons, Shackle Undead where applicable).
  • Ashtongue packs: clear before Akama unless the realm's version makes them neutral already.

Bosses

  • 1. High Warlord Naj'entus
  • 2. Supremus
  • 3. Shade of Akama
  • 4. Teron Gorefiend
  • 5. Gurtogg Bloodboil
  • 6. Reliquary of Souls
  • 7. Mother Shahraz
  • 8. Illidari Council
  • 9. Illidan Stormrage
