Raid Lead

Setup

  • P1: one tank; Shear is healed through — Holy priests and other healers on the tank.
  • P2: 2 fire resistance tanks on the Flames of Azzinoth — keep them apart and near their Blades (they enrage otherwise).
  • P4 demon form: skipped by pushing enough damage in P3 — no warlock tank needed. (If it ever triggers: warlock tank with Searing Pain at long range, kill Shadow Demons.)
  • P5: Maiev's traps are ignored unless one lands next to the tanking position.

Positioning

  • P1: ranged in the circle on the floor; parasite targets go into melee range.
  • P2: stack by group — G3 front, G5 middle, G4 back.
  • P3 + P5: raid spreads on the side of the room with no fire left over from P2.

Calls

  • Phase changes; "Eye Blast [side]".
  • Bloodlust in P3.

Wipe causes

  • Tank dying to Shear (healing not on the tank).
  • Parasites dropped in the ranged.
  • Flames too close together or too far from Blades → enrage.
  • Too little DPS in P3 → demon phase triggers.
