TL;DR

  • P1: ranged start in the circle on the floor. One tank; Shear is healed through. Stay out of his front (Draw Soul) and out of Flame Crash.
  • Parasitic Shadowfiend on you → go into melee range so the adds spawn there and die to melee.
  • P2 (65%): Illidan flies; two Flame of Azzinoth spawn. Raid stacks by group: G3 front, G5 middle, G4 back. Dodge Eye Blast; heal Dark Barrage targets.
  • P3: back on the ground. Spread on the side of the room with no fire left over from P2. Bloodlust here.
  • P4 (demon form): skipped — we push enough damage that it never triggers.
  • P5 (30%): Maiev arrives. Same spread as P3. Traps are ignored unless one lands next to the tanking position.
Affliction Warlock
  • Full single-target rotation all fight — raid DPS is what skips the demon phase.
  • Parasitic Shadowfiend on you: run into melee range, drop it, come back.
  • P2: stay with G3 (front) or your group; dodge Eye Blast.
Holy Priest
  • Pre-pull: spam rank 1 Flash Heal on the tank so Inspiration is up when the pull starts.
  • P1: tank healing. One tank, no Shear rotation — heal through Shear (PoM, Greater Heal, PW:S).
  • P3, P5: tank first; when the tank is stable, raid healing (Agonizing Flames, spread raid).
  • P2: raid healing. Dark Barrage target: PoM, Flash Heal, PW:S.
  • Groups are stacked in P2 — CoH/PoH work well.
  • If assigned to parasites + flames (P1, P3, P5): heal Parasitic Shadowfiend and Agonizing Flames targets instead of the tank; raid healing the rest of the time.
Shadow Priest
  • VT carries the raid's mana through this long fight.
  • Full single target — DPS skips the demon phase.
  • Parasite: drop it in melee range.

Strategy

P1: one tank, ranged in the floor circle, parasites dropped in melee. P2: stack by group (G3 front, G5 middle, G4 back) while the Flames are killed; dodge Eye Blast. P3: spread on the side of the room with no leftover fire from P2, Bloodlust and push hard enough to skip the demon phase. P5: same spread; ignore Maiev's traps unless one is right at the tank.

Abilities

  • P1: Shear (tank), Draw Soul, Flame Crash, Parasitic Shadowfiend.
  • P2: Eye Blast, Dark Barrage, Fireball.
  • P3: Agonizing Flames on random players.
  • P4 demon form (skipped): Shadow Blast, Flame Burst, Shadow Demons.
  • P5: Maiev's Cage Traps.
