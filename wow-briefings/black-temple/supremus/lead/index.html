Raid Lead

Setup

  • Main tank + 2–3 high-HP soakers (off-tanks/plate) in melee range for Hateful Strike (hits the highest-health melee in range besides the tank).
  • Healers: soaker healers + flex (raid) healers; soaker healers switch to raid healing in the kite phase.

Positioning

  • Tank phase: melee soakers stacked; ranged in the half circle (G3 left, G5 middle, G4 right).
  • Kite phase: no set path; tanks pick him back up as fast as possible.

Calls

  • "Kite phase — watch his target" on phase change.
  • Call out volcanoes near the raid if people are tunneling.

Wipe causes

  • Hateful Strike hitting a low-HP melee (soakers dead or out of range).
  • Raid standing in Molten Flame trails during kiting.
  • DPS too low → 15-min enrage.
