TL;DR

  • Two phases switching every ~minute.
  • Tank phase: same half circle as Naj'entus (G3 left, G5 middle, G4 right). Avoid fire on the ground.
  • Kite phase: Supremus fixates on random players and chases them; volcanoes erupt across the room. Run if it's you, dodge if it's not. Kiting is usually short — the tanks get him back quickly.
  • 15-min enrage.
Affliction Warlock
  • Cast Inferno (Summon Infernal) shortly before the pull for extra DPS.
  • DoTs carry you through the kite phase — refresh on the move.
  • Don't Life Tap carelessly; a volcano tick after a tap can kill.
  • If fixated: run, keep instant DoTs going.
Holy Priest
  • Assignment is soakers, main tank or flex (raid healing).
  • Soakers, tank phase: Hateful Strikes are very large hits — Greater Heal / Binding Heal; PoM on a soaker.
  • Kite phase: soaker healers become raid healers — fire damage on unlucky players, Renew + Flash Heal on the move.
  • Main tank: Greater Heal + PoM on the tank in the tank phase.
  • Flex: raid healing the whole fight.
  • Keep line of sight while moving.
Shadow Priest
  • Kite phase: keep SW:P and VT up, Mind Flay only when standing still.
  • If fixated, run first, DPS second.

Strategy

Tank phase: half circle by group at range, DPS. Kite phase: if fixated, run away from the raid — no set path, the tanks take him back quickly. Otherwise keep DPSing while dodging volcanoes and fire.

Abilities

  • Molten Flame — fire trails on the floor.
  • Volcanic Geyser — eruptions in the kite phase; don't stand in them.
  • Gaze — kite-phase fixate on a random player.
  • Hateful Strike — huge hit on melee soakers (healers: watch them).
