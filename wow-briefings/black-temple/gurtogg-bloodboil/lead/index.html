Gurtogg Bloodboil

Raider view

Raid Lead

Setup

  • 3 tanks for the threat game: Eject knocks back and drops threat; Acidic Wound stacks armor reduction → tanks rotate.
  • Soak rotation: G3 ×2, G4 ×2, G5 ×1 (covers one Bloodboil phase).
  • Holy priests on the soakers (CoH on the stacked group) + tank healers + Fel Rage healers.

Positioning

  • Tank in a fixed spot, melee behind/side (Arcing Smash cleave in front).
  • Ranged in one line next to the water; the soaking group one step back.

Calls

  • "G3 back" → "G4 back" → "G5 back" on the rotation.
  • "Fel Rage on [name]" → healers on them.

Wipe causes

  • Bloodboil rotation breaking (one group stacks too many DoTs).
  • Losing aggro after Eject without enough threat on the next tank.
  • Fel Rage target dying.
