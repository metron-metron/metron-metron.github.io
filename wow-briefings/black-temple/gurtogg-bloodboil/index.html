TL;DR

  • Bloodboil hits the 5 furthest players with a stacking DoT. Ranged stand in one line next to the water; the soaking group steps one step back.
  • Soak order: G3 twice, G4 twice, G5 once — that usually covers the whole Bloodboil phase.
  • Fel Rage (~30 s): Gurtogg fixates a random player who gets bonus health/armor; heal them through it.
  • Fel Geyser knocks the raid around at the start of Fel Rage.
  • Never stand in front of him. Phases alternate.
Affliction Warlock
  • Know your group's soaks (G3 = first two). Step back before your turn, step forward after.
  • Long fight — keep all DoTs and your assigned curse up.
Holy Priest
  • Usually assigned to the soakers: the soaking group is neatly stacked — CoH / PoH on them, follow the rotation G3 → G4 → G5.
  • Fel Rage target: PoM + Greater Heal spam for the ~30 s.
Shadow Priest
  • Soak with your group when it's your turn.
  • VT up all fight; Shadowfiend twice if possible.

Strategy

Ranged form one line next to the water. For each Bloodboil the soaking group steps one step back so they're the furthest five: G3 takes two, G4 two, G5 one. During Fel Rage the fixated player stays put; healers focus them, everyone else DPS.

Abilities

  • Bloodboil — stacking DoT on the 5 furthest players.
  • Fel Rage — fixate phase on a random player.
  • Fel Geyser — AoE knock-up.
  • Fel-Acid Breath — breath on a random target.
