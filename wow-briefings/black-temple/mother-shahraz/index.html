TL;DR

  • Shadow resistance gear. Each group stacks under its statue.
  • Fatal Attraction teleports 3 players together; they take big damage until they move apart — run where your WeakAura tells you.
  • Random beams of different schools hit raid members; Silencing Shriek silences.
  • Bloodlust goes out after the first Fatal Attraction.
Affliction Warlock
  • DoTs make Fatal Attraction movement cheap.
  • Prismatic Aura can make her resist shadow for a while — your damage drops, nothing to do about it.
  • Healthstone + potion — this fight hurts.
Holy Priest
  • Raid healing. Groups are stacked under statues — CoH / PoH work well; Prayer of Mending bouncing does work.
  • Fatal Attraction: lasts only 2–6 s; the shamans and CoH splash cover it — single-target heals only if one of them is low.
  • Silencing Shriek: don't start long casts right before it.
Shadow Priest
  • Keep VT up; run on Fatal Attraction.
  • Prayer of Shadow Protection for the raid.

Strategy

Groups stack under their statues in shadow resistance gear. Fatal Attraction targets run in the direction their WeakAura shows. Heal through heavy, random damage; Bloodlust after the first Fatal Attraction.

Abilities

  • Fatal Attraction — 3 players teleported together, damage until separated.
  • Beams — random-target damage of various schools.
  • Silencing Shriek — AoE silence.
  • Prismatic Aura — shifts which damage school she resists, so your damage changes; it doesn't change the damage you take.
  • Frenzy late in the fight.
