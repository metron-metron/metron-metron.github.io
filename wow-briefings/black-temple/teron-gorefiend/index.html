TL;DR

  • Ranged stack on top of the stairs.

Strategy

Ranged stack on top of the stairs. Shadow of Death targets head to the far back corner about 10 s before they die and get a Soulstone from the warlocks. As a ghost, root the Constructs with Spirit Chains and kill them with Spirit Volley in the corner.

Abilities

  • Shadow of Death — kills you after ~55 s; then you're a ghost.
  • Ghost bar: Spirit Lance (slow), Spirit Chains (root), Spirit Volley (AoE), Spirit Strike, Spirit Shield.
  • Incinerate — fire DoT, magic, dispellable.
  • Crushing Shadows — shadow damage on several players.
  • Doom Blossoms — shoot shadow bolts at players.
