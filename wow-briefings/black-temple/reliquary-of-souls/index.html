TL;DR

  • Three bosses in a row, with Enslaved Soul adds between — kill them fast.
  • Essence of Suffering: Aura of Suffering stops all healing. DPS race — don't stand closest unless you're a soaker.
  • Essence of Desire: Aura of Desire — damage you deal also hurts you; watch your HP.
  • Essence of Anger: stacking Aura of Anger shadow damage and Spite — burn.
  • Raid stacks loosely at range the whole fight.
Affliction Warlock
  • P1: Life Tap freely — there's almost no other damage, you don't need the HP.
  • P2: Aura of Desire hurts you as you deal damage; Siphon Life helps.
  • P3: burn — all DoTs, Shadow Ward on cooldown, Healthstone.
Holy Priest
  • P1: healing is blocked — DPS (SW:P, Holy Fire, Mind Blast, Smite), Power Word: Shield (absorbs still work) and dispel Soul Drain.
  • P2: raid healing.
  • P3: raid healing; steady ramp of shadow damage on everyone — PoH/CoH spam.
Shadow Priest
  • P2: VT heals you while you do damage.
  • P3: burn, Shadowfiend, VT for the healers.

Strategy

Raid stacks loosely at range. P1: soakers stand closest; everyone else DPS. Enslaved Souls between phases die fast. P2: steady DPS while watching your health. P3: all-out burn as raid damage ramps.

Abilities

  • Suffering: Aura of Suffering, fixates the closest player, Soul Drain.
  • Desire: Aura of Desire, Rune Shield, Deaden, Spirit Shock.
  • Anger: Aura of Anger, Seethe, Soul Scream, Spite.
