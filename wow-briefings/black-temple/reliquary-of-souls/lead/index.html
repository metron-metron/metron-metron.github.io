Raid Lead

Setup

  • P1: soakers (warriors with Shield Wall etc.) stand closest — the boss fixates the nearest player.
  • P2: interrupt rotation on Spirit Shock (must interrupt); Spell Reflect/Grounding for Deaden; mages/shamans purge/steal Rune Shield.
  • Shadow resistance (or potions) for P3.
  • Priests: P1 DPS + PW:S + dispel Soul Drain; P2/P3 raid healing.

Positioning

  • Raid stacks loosely at range all fight; P1 soakers in melee.

Calls

  • "Souls incoming" between phases — AoE/DPS on them.
  • "Interrupt" rotation on Spirit Shock.
  • P3: Bloodlust/Heroism here.

Wipe causes

  • P1 soakers dying (no healing possible).
  • Missed Spirit Shock interrupts.
  • P3 too slow — Aura of Anger stacks out-heal the raid.
