Overview

Illidan's fortress in Shadowmoon Valley. Nine bosses. Naj'entus first; then Supremus → Shade of Akama, the Teron Gorefiend / Gurtogg wing, Reliquary of Souls, Mother Shahraz, the Illidari Council, and Illidan.

Trash

  • Lots of demon trash: Banish and Fear are valuable — follow the CC marks.
  • Ashtongue packs become friendly after the Shade of Akama event.
  • Pack-specific notes once we've cleared it: TBD.

Consumables
