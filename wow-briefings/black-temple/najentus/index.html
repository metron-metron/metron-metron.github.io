Affliction Warlock
  • Full DoT rotation + Shadow Bolt.
  • Tidal Shield makes the boss immune — refresh DoTs right after it breaks.
  • Don't Life Tap below ~70% HP: Needle Spine + Tidal Burst can kill you.
  • Healthstone ready.
Holy Priest
  • Spread damage all fight: Prayer of Mending on cooldown, CoH as the main heal.
  • Usually assigned to groups 3 + 4: CoH on whichever of them is hurt most; keep both in range.
  • Usually only one Tidal Shield per kill — have the raid topped before the burst; keep CoH going through it.
  • Impaled players are freed within seconds by whoever stands near them — healing them is rarely needed.
Shadow Priest
  • VT and SW:P up; your VT mana carries the healers through the bursts.
  • Shadowfiend early; it's back for the second half.
  • Re-DoT after every Tidal Shield.

Strategy

Boss is tanked where he stands. The raid forms a half circle by group, each player spread inside their group. Whoever is closest frees an impaled player. When the shield goes up, spine holders wait for the RL's call, then throw; everyone is topped before the burst. Simple DPS race.

Abilities

  • Needle Spine — spines on random players, splashing anyone near them.
  • Impaling Spine — pins a player with a DoT until someone clicks the spine. Freeing them gives you a Naj'entus Spine.
  • Tidal Shield — boss immune; broken by a thrown spine.
  • Tidal Burst — raid-wide frost damage when the shield breaks.
