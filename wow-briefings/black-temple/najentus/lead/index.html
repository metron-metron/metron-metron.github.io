High Warlord Naj'entus

Raider view

Raid Lead

Setup

  • 1 tank, no swaps. 5–6 healers; Holy priests assigned to two groups each (e.g. G3 + G4).
  • Spine throwing: anyone holding a spine, on the RL's call.

Positioning

  • Tank him where he stands.
  • Half circle by group: G3 left, G5 middle, G4 right. Spread inside groups, but within CoH range of each other.

Calls

  • "Top the raid" when the shield goes up.
  • "Safe to throw" once the raid is topped.

Wipe causes

  • Stacked players chaining Needle Spine.
  • No one throwing → long shield, enrage at 8 min.
