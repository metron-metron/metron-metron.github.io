Raid Lead

Setup

  • Gathios: warrior/paladin tank — focus target.
  • Veras and Malande tanked right next to Gathios so melee cleave hits all three. Interrupt Malande's Circle of Healing (rogue/warrior rotation).
  • Zerevor: often tanked by a mage (Spellsteal Dampen Magic, range). Interrupt Arcane Bolt where possible.
  • Holy priests: raid healing + Veras' poison targets.

Positioning

  • Gathios, Veras and Malande stacked; Zerevor separate.
  • Raid loosely around the tanks.

Calls

  • "Reflective Shield on Malande — stop damage on her."
  • Bloodlust at the start (shared HP).

Wipe causes

  • Malande healing the pool (missed interrupts).
  • Zerevor loose on the ranged.
