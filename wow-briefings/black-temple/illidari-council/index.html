TL;DR

  • Four members: Gathios the Shatterer, High Nethermancer Zerevor, Lady Malande, Veras Darkshadow.
  • Shared health pool — damage on any of them counts; they die together.
  • Focus Gathios; Veras and Malande are tanked close to him so cleave hits all three. Raid stands loosely around the tanks.
  • Move out of Zerevor's Blizzard and Flamestrike and Gathios' Consecration.
  • Malande's Reflective Shield reflects damage — no damage into her while it's up.
Affliction Warlock
  • DoTs on all four — the shared health pool makes multi-DoT pure value.
  • Filler (Shadow Bolt) on Gathios, the focus target.
  • No damage into Reflective Shield; watch threat on the casters.
Holy Priest
  • Raid healing plus Veras' Deadly Poison targets; the tanks take a real share of your healing too.
  • Poison target: PW:S right away — Deadly Poison lasts only 3–4 s, no time for Greater Heal. Flash Heal if they are low.
  • Zerevor's AoEs on ranged: CoH.
Shadow Priest
  • DoTs on all four; Mind Flay on Gathios.
  • No damage into Reflective Shield.

Strategy

Gathios, Veras and Malande are tanked close together; the raid stands loosely around the tanks. DPS focuses Gathios while cleave hits the other two. Healers cover the raid and Veras' poison targets.

Abilities

  • Zerevor: Arcane Bolt, Blizzard, Flamestrike — hit ranged.
  • Malande: Empowered Smite, Reflective Shield.
  • Gathios: Consecration, Hammer of Justice (stun).
  • Veras: Vanish, Deadly Poison and Envenom on random targets.
