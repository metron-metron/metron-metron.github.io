Shade of Akama

Raider view

Raid Lead

Setup

  • Keep one trash mob alive before the pull and park it in the middle of the Channelers, under the Shade (Seed target).
  • Warlocks: Seed off the trash mob. Mages: Arcane Explosion on the Channelers. Melee: Channelers counterclockwise.
  • Tanks: pick up the door adds and hold them.

Positioning

  • Phase 1: ranged/casters around the Channeler circle in Seed/Arcane Explosion range.
  • Phase 2: whole raid moves to Akama; tanks bring the adds there for AoE.

Calls

  • Start: talk to Akama.
  • "Channelers down — to Akama, AoE."
  • "Burn the Shade — ignore adds."

Wipe causes

  • Trash mob dies early → no Seed target, slow Channeler kill.
  • Tanks lose door adds onto the casters.
  • Akama dying before the Shade.
