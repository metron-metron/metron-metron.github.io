Archimonde

Raider view

Raid Lead

Setup

  • 1 tank (+ backup); a Holy priest keeps Fear Ward on the tank.
  • Raid plan with marked positions: shamans in a half circle around the boss, each party around its shaman for Tremor Totem.
  • Holy priests: raid healing (Renew).
  • Decursers for Grip of the Legion.
  • Someone must always be in melee range — otherwise he casts Finger of Death.

Positioning

  • Tank in place; shamans in a half circle around him, parties stacked on their shaman (raid plan).

Calls

  • "Doomfire [side]," "Air Burst — Tears!"
  • Bloodlust once everyone is in position; the fight ends at 10 min with wisps.

Wipe causes

  • Doomfire deaths → Soul Charges hit the raid.
  • Nobody in melee → Finger of Death.
