Hyjal Summit

Raid Lead

Composition

  • 25 players, ~3 tanks + 1 for Infernals/Doomguards, 6–7 healers for wave AoE.
  • Bring decursers (Archimonde, Banshees), Fear Ward/Tremor for Archimonde, Shamans for Tremor.

Waves

  • Raid spot: LOS behind the left tower at the base entrance; tanks pull the waves there.
  • Assign wave tanks and a ranged team on flying mobs (Gargoyles, Frost Wyrms).
  • Kill priority per wave: Necromancers (summon skeletons) → Banshees (interrupt Banshee Wail / curse) → Abominations → Ghouls/Fiends.
  • Thrall's base: Giant Infernals fall onto the base — tank them away from the raid; Fel Stalkers need fast kills.
  • Call AoE windows (Seed/Blizzard/Consecration) once the tank has the pack.
  • Call drink breaks — between waves there's often 20–40 s.

Resets

  • If the raid wipes during waves, the event resets to the last completed boss. Wiping on a boss resets its waves.
