Overview

Wave-defense raid in the Caverns of Time. The first four bosses each come after 8 trash waves at a base: Rage Winterchill and Anetheron at Jaina's (Alliance) base, Kaz'rogal and Azgalor at Thrall's (Horde) base. Archimonde comes last at the World Tree. Waves arrive on a timer, but we clear each one before the next — nothing spills into the next wave or the boss.

Trash waves

  • Raid stands at a set spot at both bases: line of sight behind the left tower at the base entrance — the waves come to us.
  • Affliction: Seed of Corruption freely. Holy: raid healing.
  • Constant AoE damage on the raid; waves are long — pace your mana across 8 waves + boss.
  • Stay at range: Abominations leave poison clouds, Crypt Fiends web nearby players, Banshees curse and silence.
  • Frost Wyrms and Gargoyles fly — only ranged can hit them. Expect to be called onto them.
  • At Thrall's base Fel Stalkers mana-burn casters — stay spread from other casters.
  • Drink between waves whenever there's a gap.

Consumables

Flask, food buff, mana/healing potions, Dark/Demonic Runes. For Archimonde: Tears of the Goddess (handed out before the fight).
