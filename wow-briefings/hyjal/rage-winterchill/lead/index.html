Raid Lead

Setup

  • 1 tank on the boss, 1 for leftover wave adds.
  • Assign dispellers (priests/paladins). Holy priests on raid healing, one per group (G3/G4).
  • Frost Armor on the boss slows melee — mages/shamans steal/purge it.

Positioning

  • Tank him where he lands at the base.
  • Groups: G3 tree, G4 smithy, G5 ballista.
  • Melee stay in D&D — healers heal through it.

Calls

  • "D&D — ranged move" if ranged stand in it.

Wipe causes

  • Melee dying in D&D (healers not on melee).
