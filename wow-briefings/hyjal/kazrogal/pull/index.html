TL;DR

  • At Thrall's base after 8 waves. Fought at the base entrance; ranged stand across the path.
  • Bloodlust at the start.
  • Mark of Kaz'rogal drains mana from all mana users periodically. If your mana hits 0, you explode and damage everyone near you — about to run dry? Run away from the raid.
  • The interval between Marks shrinks — DPS race.
  • Warlocks Life Tap, Shadow Priests VT, everyone else potions/runes.

Affliction Warlock

  • Life Tap to keep mana above 0 — you only explode if a Mark empties you
  • Pure DPS race

Holy Priest

  • Usually DPS — respec to Shadow for this fight
  • Going OOM: run away from the raid before the Mark empties you

Shadow Priest

  • VT keeps the raid's mana up — crucial here
  • Shadowfiend + potion when Marks get close
