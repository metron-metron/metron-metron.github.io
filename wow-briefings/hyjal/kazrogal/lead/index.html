Kaz'rogal

Raider view

Raid Lead

Setup

  • 1 tank (+ backup). Melee get War Stomp stuns — fine.
  • Maximise DPS: Bloodlust at the start; the fight gets harder the longer it lasts.
  • Holy priests usually respec to DPS.

Positioning

  • Fight him at the entrance to the base; ranged across the path.
  • OOM players just move away from the raid.

Calls

  • "Mark in 5 — check mana."

Wipe causes

  • OOM players exploding in the raid.
  • Too slow — Marks every few seconds drain everyone.
