Setup

  • 1 main tank, tanks Azgalor at Thrall (Cleave — melee from behind).
  • 1 Doom tank next to the tauren warrior NPCs holds the Lesser Doomguards; the NPCs kill them.
  • Warlocks Soulstone Doomed players in a pre-set order.
  • Holy priests on raid healing.

Positioning

  • Azgalor at Thrall; groups G3 left, G5 middle, G4 right.
  • Doomed players go to the Doom tank.

Calls

  • Bloodlust once he's in position.
  • "Doom on [name] — go to the Doom tank," next warlock Soulstones.

Wipe causes

  • Doomguards loose in the raid (Doom tank dies / Doomed player dies in the raid).
  • Missed Soulstones → losing players over time.
