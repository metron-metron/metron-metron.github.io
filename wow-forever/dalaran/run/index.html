City of Dalaran

TL;DR

  • New Forever dungeon: an instanced Dalaran reached through the sewers. Horde need the Dalaran Sewer Key from a quest chain starting with Magus Wordeen Voidglare in Tarren Mill.
  • Arcane damage, mana drain, polymorphs: bring water, interrupt Mana Phantoms, dispel Manamorph.
  • 7 bosses + secret Lyn the Ignored (needs the Tome of Dalaran from SFK). After the sewers, street bosses in any order; the run ends at the Violet Citadel.
  • Final boss Shade of the Archmage: mass polymorph at 0 mana, then Evocation.

Discipline Priest

  • Mostly arcane damage: Mana Devourer Debilitate drains 200 mana from everyone — bring water.
  • Arcanic Enigma Manamorph is a dispellable polymorph: Dispel Magic it.
  • Shade of the Archmage polymorphs the whole party at 0 mana, then Evocates: top everyone off before.

Affliction Warlock

  • Phantasm Reflection Field reflects magic: stop casting on shielded targets.
  • Kirin Tor Necromancer skeletons: DoTs on the necromancer, not the skeletons.
  • Fel Ancient Entangle roots 10 s; Fel Timbers pool — move.

Warrior Tank

  • Saturated Remnants hit with arcane: you can't block them.
  • Unstable Sentinel and Fragmented Sentries: Arcing Smash — face away; stun before Malfunction.
  • Shade: move him off his cast spot when Bounding Mana launches.

Route

  1. Get the key: Tarren Mill, Magus Wordeen Voidglare → Key to the City → (optional Prison Break In, Stone Tokens) → Dalaran Patrols → Blood in the Streets → Heart of Disruption → Archmage Modera gives the Dalaran Sewer Key. Once you have access, The Sepulcher (Silverpine) is the faster start — swim across.
  2. Entrance: outside the city, north-west side, gate opened with the key (WT: ~69.3, 45.6). You start in the Cantrips and Crows bar in the sewers.
  3. Sewers (skeletons): cross the bridge and turn right, through three large pipes to the Underbelly: Atrexis the Grave Knight patrols with 3 Kirin Tor Necromancers — clear a couple first.
  4. (Secret) A side passage to a square room with a summoning circle: Lyn the Ignored, summoned with a Tome of Dalaran (Black Tome from Arugal's room in SFK, empowered at the Ambermill Leyline Focus in Silverpine). The Tome isn't used up (3 min cooldown).
  5. Up the ramp out of the sewers onto the streets (Krasus' Landing area). Bosses in any order; suggested:
    1. Violet Hold — clear it, then Mana Devourer.
    2. Krasus' Landing — Arcane Anomaly.
    3. Runeweaver Square — Fel Ancient (talk to the NPC who lowers the barriers: Grindle Firespark per Method, Magus Fansy Goodbringer per WT).
    4. Path of Renewal — Arcanic Enigma (patrols with two Mana Phantoms).
    5. Magus Commerce Exchange — Unstable Sentinel.
  6. Talk to Archmage Modera on the Violet Citadel steps — she ports you to the Violet Chamber: Shade of the Archmage. Run ends.
  • Shortcut: a locked door left of the sewer bridge opens outside the Violet Citadel (skips almost everything). How to open it: not documented.

Bosses

Atrexis the Grave Knight

Clear some Necromancers first. Kill the first raised skeletons, then the boss; Purge or wait out Unholy Frenzy.
Disarm + Frenzy spikes the tank — Shield.
DoTs on Atrexis; Corruption on skeletons only if they reach the healer.
Disarmed: Sunder/Revenge/Shield Bash still work. Thunder Clap for skeletons.

Lyn the Ignored · rare

Optional (needs the Tome). Decurse the tank if anyone can; spread for the volley.
Group-wide shadow damage: Renew the group, not just the tank.
You can't decurse; ask for it or heal through.

Mana Devourer

Clear the Violet Hold first or the mobs join. Heal check — kill fast.
Mana is the fight; drink before the pull.
Life Tap after, not during.
Straight tank and spank.

Arcane Anomaly

Stay close to the boss — Focal Blast is a 3 s channelled beam that sweeps; distance gets you hit.
Stand near the boss, out of the sweep.
Close range for once.
Tank where the group can stack near him.

Fel Ancient

Lower the barriers first. Move out of the green pool; the rooted player waits it out (Blessing of Freedom removes it).
Heal the rooted player; step out of pools.
Drag him off pools under the group.

Arcanic Enigma

Interrupt and stun its two Mana Phantoms first (Mana Burn). Dispel Manamorph. Kill Manalings.
Dispel Magic on Manamorph; out of range of Mana Burn.
Phantoms first.
Shield Bash the Phantoms.

Unstable Sentinel

Face it away (Arcing Smash per Method). Non-tanks leave 25 yd during Malfunction (arcane pulse every 2 s); stun before the cast stops it.
Heal from beyond 25 yd.
Concussion Blow (30, talent) before Malfunction.

Shade of the Archmage

Move him off the spot when Bounding Mana launches (it returns to its origin). Stay out of Arcane Explosion range (20 yd). At 0 mana he Mass Polymorphs the party, then Evocation — fight restarts.
Top everyone off before his mana runs out; drink is impossible while polymorphed.
Range; DoTs keep ticking through the polymorph.
Reposition for every Bounding Mana; Taunt after the polymorph.
