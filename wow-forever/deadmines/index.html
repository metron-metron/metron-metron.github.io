The Deadmines

Run view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Westfall (Moonbrook), Alliance territory. Horde get no regular Deadmines quests; it's a long trip (zeppelin to Grom'gol, then north). Do it for loot, not XP.
  • Dense humanoid packs: Defias, goblins, a few ogres. Interrupt Defias Evoker Flamestrike.
  • Sneed: Shredder first, then Sneed with a fresh threat table.
  • Ship finale: Mr. Smite → Captain Greenskin → Edwin VanCleef. Cookie optional.
  • VanCleef calls 2 more adds at 50% — stun him as he drops below 50% to stop it (Method).
Discipline Priest · Metrolyn
  • Tight packs, lots of stuns on the tank: Shield is your best spell here.
Affliction Warlock · Metronom
  • Don't Fear in the mine corridors.
Warrior Tank · Metronis
  • Mark: Evoker → Overseer → the rest.

Route

  1. Moonbrook, Defias Hideout (south-west, ~42, 72); follow the cave maze to the portal.
  2. Mine tunnels. Rhahk'Zor guards a door, with two Defias Watchmen.
  3. Past the door, left at the junction: Miner Johnson (rare, may be absent).
  4. Sneed's room: clear the Goblin Woodcarvers first, then Sneed's Shredder.
  5. Gilnid's room (the foundry): clear one side, then Gilnid with his Craftsman and Engineer.
  6. Large cavern with the docks and the ship. Mr. Smite guards the ship.
  7. On deck: Captain Greenskin with his crew — easy to pull by accident.
  8. Edwin VanCleef (Greenskin must be dead).
  9. Cookie (optional): after Smite turn left aboard ship, or after VanCleef drop to the lower deck toward the gangplank.
  10. Exit by the tunnel at the back; you come out in the hills behind Moonbrook.

Bosses

Rhahk'Zor · 19

Pull him without the two Defias Watchmen if you can; otherwise kill the Watchmen first (or CC one).

  • Rhahk'Zor Slam: 330 damage + 3 s stun on the tank.
Discipline: Shield the tank; the Slam stun is when damage spikes.
Affliction: Watchmen first if they come.
Tank: Taunt back whatever the Watchmen hit while you're stunned.

Miner Johnson (rare) · 19 · rare

Kill him first, then his Defias Miners.

  • Pierce Armor: −75% armour on the tank for 20 s.
Discipline: Tank takes much more while Pierce Armor is up — Heal, not Lesser Heal.
Tank: Shield Block during Pierce Armor.

Sneed's Shredder and Sneed

Clear the room. Kill the Shredder; Sneed pops out with a new threat table — tank picks him up at once.

  • Shredder: Terrify (4 s fear) and Distracting Pain (−35% cast speed). Sneed: Disarm on whoever has threat.
Discipline: Will of the Forsaken out of Terrify; heal the Disarmed tank.
Affliction: Stop DoTs when the Shredder is low so you don't pull Sneed onto yourself.
Tank: Taunt Sneed when he jumps out. Disarmed: Sunder, Revenge, Shield Bash still work.

Gilnid · 20

Gilnid with an elite Goblin Craftsman and Engineer (Remote-Controlled Golem). Boss first (Method) or adds first (WT) — default: Engineer and its golem first.

  • Molten Metal: fire DoT, −54% attack speed, slows movement, lowers threat.
Discipline: Steady tank damage; Renew.
Affliction: DoTs on all.
Tank: Molten Metal lowers your threat — Sunder more, Taunt if the boss turns.

Mr. Smite · 20

Kill both stealthed Defias Blackguards first. Two stomps: he swaps to a 2H mace (Thrash), then dual-wield (higher damage) — tank defensives for the last phase.

  • Smite Stomp (stun), Smite Slam (220 + 3 s stun), Thrash.
Discipline: Big tank heals in the dual-wield phase; Shield during the stun.
Affliction: Blackguards first.
Tank: Shield Block / Shield Wall (28) for the dual-wield phase.

Captain Greenskin · 20

Kill order: Defias Companion (non-elite) → Greenskin → Squallshaper and Pirate.

  • Poisoned Harpoon: big nature DoT on the tank for 1 min. Cleave.
Discipline: Renew covers the Harpoon ticks; Heal on top.
Affliction: Companion, then the boss.
Tank: Face him away (Cleave). Thunder Clap to hold the crew.

Edwin VanCleef · 21

Taunt the first two Blackguards off the healer; at 50% he calls two more — stun him just before 50% to stop it (Method). Burn VanCleef.

  • Thrash, VanCleef's Allies.
Discipline: Heaviest tank damage of the run once four Blackguards are up.
Affliction: All on VanCleef unless the healer needs adds dead.
Tank: Concussion Blow (30, talent) to stop the 50% summon if you have it.

Cookie · 20

Optional. Interrupt Cookie's Cooking (self-heal). Don't pull with trash.

  • Acid Splash: nature DoT around him.
Tank: Shield Bash his heal.

Trash

  • Defias Evoker — Flamestrike on an area (interrupt, move out).
  • Defias Overseer — Battle Command, attack-speed buff for nearby allies.
  • Defias Taskmaster — Piercing Shot, −580 armour on the tank for 15 s.
  • Goblin Woodcarver — Axe Toss (3 s stun), Cleave.
  • Goblin Craftsman / Engineer — Melt Ore (fire, slow) / summons a Remote-Controlled Golem.
  • Defias Squallshaper — Fire Blast on the tank, Chilled.
  • Defias Pirate — brings a Bloodsail Companion.
