The Deadmines

TL;DR

  • Westfall (Moonbrook), Alliance territory. Horde get no regular Deadmines quests; it's a long trip (zeppelin to Grom'gol, then north). Do it for loot, not XP.
  • Dense humanoid packs: Defias, goblins, a few ogres. Interrupt Defias Evoker Flamestrike.
  • Sneed: Shredder first, then Sneed with a fresh threat table.
  • Ship finale: Mr. Smite → Captain Greenskin → Edwin VanCleef. Cookie optional.
  • VanCleef calls 2 more adds at 50% — stun him as he drops below 50% to stop it (Method).

Discipline Priest

  • Greenskin's Poisoned Harpoon ticks nature damage on the tank for a minute — keep Renew on.
  • VanCleef summons two more Blackguards at 50%: big heals then.

Affliction Warlock

  • Packs are tight humanoids: Fear is a pull risk. CC (Succubus, 20+) one add on Rhahk'Zor or Greenskin if asked.
  • Kill Greenskin's Defias Companion first.

Warrior Tank

  • Sneed: after the Shredder dies, Sneed has a fresh threat table and Disarms whoever has aggro — Taunt.
  • Mr. Smite: Smite Slam stuns; keep defensives for his dual-wield phase.
  • Greenskin is easy to pull by accident — clear the ship deck slowly.

Route

  1. Moonbrook, Defias Hideout (south-west, ~42, 72); follow the cave maze to the portal.
  2. Mine tunnels. Rhahk'Zor guards a door, with two Defias Watchmen.
  3. Past the door, left at the junction: Miner Johnson (rare, may be absent).
  4. Sneed's room: clear the Goblin Woodcarvers first, then Sneed's Shredder.
  5. Gilnid's room (the foundry): clear one side, then Gilnid with his Craftsman and Engineer.
  6. Large cavern with the docks and the ship. Mr. Smite guards the ship.
  7. On deck: Captain Greenskin with his crew — easy to pull by accident.
  8. Edwin VanCleef (Greenskin must be dead).
  9. Cookie (optional): after Smite turn left aboard ship, or after VanCleef drop to the lower deck toward the gangplank.
  10. Exit by the tunnel at the back; you come out in the hills behind Moonbrook.

Bosses

Rhahk'Zor

Pull him without the two Defias Watchmen if you can; otherwise kill the Watchmen first (or CC one).
Shield the tank; the Slam stun is when damage spikes.
Watchmen first if they come.
Taunt back whatever the Watchmen hit while you're stunned.

Miner Johnson (rare) · rare

Kill him first, then his Defias Miners.
Tank takes much more while Pierce Armor is up — Heal, not Lesser Heal.
Shield Block during Pierce Armor.

Sneed's Shredder and Sneed

Clear the room. Kill the Shredder; Sneed pops out with a new threat table — tank picks him up at once.
Will of the Forsaken out of Terrify; heal the Disarmed tank.
Stop DoTs when the Shredder is low so you don't pull Sneed onto yourself.
Taunt Sneed when he jumps out. Disarmed: Sunder, Revenge, Shield Bash still work.

Gilnid

Gilnid with an elite Goblin Craftsman and Engineer (Remote-Controlled Golem). Boss first (Method) or adds first (WT) — default: Engineer and its golem first.
Steady tank damage; Renew.
DoTs on all.
Molten Metal lowers your threat — Sunder more, Taunt if the boss turns.

Mr. Smite

Kill both stealthed Defias Blackguards first. Two stomps: he swaps to a 2H mace (Thrash), then dual-wield (higher damage) — tank defensives for the last phase.
Big tank heals in the dual-wield phase; Shield during the stun.
Blackguards first.
Shield Block / Shield Wall (28) for the dual-wield phase.

Captain Greenskin

Kill order: Defias Companion (non-elite) → Greenskin → Squallshaper and Pirate.
Renew covers the Harpoon ticks; Heal on top.
Companion, then the boss.
Face him away (Cleave). Thunder Clap to hold the crew.

Edwin VanCleef

Taunt the first two Blackguards off the healer; at 50% he calls two more — stun him just before 50% to stop it (Method). Burn VanCleef.
Heaviest tank damage of the run once four Blackguards are up.
All on VanCleef unless the healer needs adds dead.
Concussion Blow (30, talent) to stop the 50% summon if you have it.

Cookie

Optional. Interrupt Cookie's Cooking (self-heal). Don't pull with trash.
Shield Bash his heal.
