Forever Dungeons
Wetlands · level 26–31 · unchecked

Excavation Site: Wetlands

Run view ⤢

TL;DR

  • New Forever dungeon in the Wetlands. No Horde town nearby: open the Hammerfall flight path (Arathi) and walk south.
  • 3 loot bosses: Saltspine (crocolisk), Shadetooth (raptor), Relic Guardian (end). Highland Horror is an Alliance-only quest boss.
  • Bleeds, poisons, diseases all run — Cure Disease, heal through the rest.
  • Raptors hide in the grass and call for help: slow pulls.
  • Shadetooth fears into hidden packs: Fear Ward the tank, or clear the grass first.
Discipline Priest · Metrolyn
  • Cure Disease often; Fear Ward for Shadetooth.
Affliction Warlock · Metronom
  • Fear isn't safe in the thicket (hidden packs).
Warrior Tank · Metronis
  • Slow, deliberate pulls in the thicket; Thunder Clap + Demoralizing Shout on raptor packs.

Route

  1. From Hammerfall south into the Wetlands; Meeting Stone at ~53.9, 65.4, entrance up the hill at ~48.1, 56.5 (spider cave by Thelgen Rock; non-elite spiders on the path).
  2. Down a slope into the Lost Marsh: crocolisks, turtles, Marsh Skulkers.
  3. Saltspine patrols the marsh edge — pull him onto dry ground.
  4. Stalker's Thicket: raptors in tall grass, many stealthed; they call for help. Clear most of it.
  5. Shadetooth patrols the whole thicket with two Thicket Hunters.
  6. Lost Dig Site: Dragonmaw orcs.
  7. (Alliance quest) Highland Horror north of the dig site, surrounded by Oil Slimes — skip.
  8. South past Highland Lurkers and Spiders to the Site of the Guardian.
  9. Relic Guardian: dormant until someone uses the control panel beside the column to its right. Run ends.

Bosses

Saltspine · 28

Pull onto dry ground; ranged and healer stay out of Dust Storm (10 yd).

  • Dust Storm: physical AoE, lowers hit chance (Method: tank threat). Fatal Bite: drains 2537 health and heals him. Tendon Rip: 71% slow.
Discipline: Heal from beyond 10 yd; Fatal Bite is a big tank spike.
Affliction: Range; DoTs.
Tank: Sunder through the missed attacks.

Shadetooth · 29

Kill the two Thicket Hunters first. Fear Ward / Tremor Totem the tank, or clear the grass first so the fear can't drag anyone into packs. Save cooldowns for the 20% enrage.

  • Terrifying Roar: AoE fear 5 s. Rend: tank bleed. Enrage at 20%: +30% attack speed.
Discipline: Fear Ward on the tank before the pull; WotF for yourself. Mana for the enrage.
Affliction: WotF if feared toward the grass.
Tank: Shield Block and Shield Wall (28) for the enrage.

Highland Horror

Alliance quest boss only — skip.

Relic Guardian · 31

Activate at the panel. Tank faces it away, no one near ledges; ranged stay out of melee range.

  • Thunderclap (attack slow), Arcing Smash (frontal cleave), Knock Away (threat reduction), War Stomp (AoE knockback + 5 s stun).
Discipline: Big tank-and-healer check. Range; Shield the tank after War Stomp.
Affliction: Stay at range. It can be slowed and stunned.
Tank: Knock Away cuts your threat: Taunt and Sunder after. Back away from edges.

Trash

  • Young Riptooth — packs of 3, Fatal Bite. One at a time; raptors hit very hard.
  • Thicket Lurker / Stalker — invisible in the grass until aggro; Rend Flesh.
  • Thicket Hunter — Infected Wound (disease, more physical damage taken). Cure.
  • Thicket Matriarch — Rend Flesh.
  • Marsh Skulker — Muculent Rot (−Spirit, −Stamina).
  • Elder Crocolisk — Tendon Rip (71% slow).
  • Dragonmaw Saboteur — bombs on an area; move after the channel.
  • Dragonmaw Warder — Mortal Strike (−50% healing), Net (10 s root).
  • Dragonmaw Thaumaturgist — Immolate, Curse of Weakness, summons a fire elemental (kill it first).
  • Highland Lurker — poison + Highland Venom disease. Highland Spider — non-elite packs of 3.
