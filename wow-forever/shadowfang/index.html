Silverpine Forest · level 22–30 · unchecked

Shadowfang Keep

Run view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Silverpine Forest, north of Pyrewood. Horde home turf: The Sepulcher flight path.
  • Kill Rethilgore first to open the courtyard; then the keep, boss by boss up to Archmage Arugal.
  • Curses everywhere (Haunting Spirits, Forsaken Skills) — neither of our classes removes curses.
  • Springvale: Wailing Guardsman first, interrupt Holy Light. Silverlaine: −75% healing on the tank.
  • Arugal: shadow bolts for 1399, mind control, teleports. Line of sight, shadow resistance helps.
Discipline Priest · Metrolyn
  • No curse removal here: heal through Haunting Spirits and Veil of Shadow.
  • Flash Heal (20) for Arugal's Void Bolts.
Affliction Warlock · Metronom
  • Moonwalkers immune to magic for 10 s: hit something else.
  • The Orb of Soran'ruk warlock quest drops here (Large Soran'ruk Fragment, Shadowfang Darksoul).
Warrior Tank · Metronis
  • Mark: Wailing Guardsman → Lupine Horror → casters.
  • Concussion Blow (30, talent) stops a Springvale Holy Light when Shield Bash is on cooldown.

Route

  1. Entrance in the mountains north of Pyrewood Village (~44.5, 68).
  2. Rethilgore with two Bleak Worgs and a Whitescalp in the cells — kill him to open the courtyard door.
  3. Courtyard: Fel Steeds (stuns). Stables at the side (rare Fel Steed / Shadow Charger).
  4. Into the keep: Razorclaw the Butcher in the kitchen.
  5. Baron Silverlaine in a room full of adds — clear them first.
  6. Commander Springvale with a Haunted Servitor and a Wailing Guardsman.
  7. Odo the Blindwatcher with Vile Bats. Rare Deathsworn Captain in the area after Odo.
  8. Fenrus the Devourer guards the way to Arugal's chambers (no adds).
  9. Wolf Master Nandos with four wolves, guarding Arugal's door; killing the wolves aggroes him.
  10. Archmage Arugal in his room with three other worgen — kill them first. Run ends.

Bosses

Rethilgore · 20

Kill the adds first, then him; stun when you can.

  • Soul Drain: roots a player 10 s and drains 178 health.
Discipline: Watch the drained player.
Affliction: DoT the worgs, then the boss.
Tank: Thunder Clap + Demoralizing Shout for the adds.

Razorclaw the Butcher · 22

Kill his adds (marked), then tank and spank.

  • Butcher Drain: drains the tank's resource, often.
Discipline: Easy fight.
Tank: He drains your rage — keep Bloodrage for it.

Baron Silverlaine · 24

Clear the whole room first. Interrupt Veil of Shadow if possible; stun him.

  • Veil of Shadow: −75% healing taken on the tank for 15 s.
Discipline: Shield the tank before Veil lands; Renew still ticks at a quarter.
Affliction: Steady DoTs.
Tank: Shield Bash Veil; Shield Block while it's on you.

Commander Springvale · 24

Kill the Wailing Guardsman first (5 s silence in melee), then the Servitor, then Springvale. At ~30% he bubbles then casts Holy Light — interrupt it.

  • Hammer of Justice (4 s stun), Holy Light, Divine Shield (10 s immunity).
Discipline: Stay out of melee (silence). Shield the tank during Hammer of Justice.
Affliction: Keep DoTs ready for when Divine Shield ends.
Tank: Shield Bash Holy Light as soon as the shield drops.

Odo the Blindwatcher · 24

Tank holds the Vile Bats facing away (they cleave and disarm). Save damage for below 50% — Howling Rage grows his damage as he loses health.

Discipline: Damage ramps late: be at full mana for the last half.
Affliction: DoTs early, burst late.
Tank: Thunder Clap on the bats; defensives below 50%.

Deathsworn Captain (rare) · 25 · rare

Don't pull with trash. Tank faces him away; nobody stands in front.

  • Cleave, Hamstring.

Fenrus the Devourer · 25

DPS check, no adds.

  • Toxic Saliva: nature DoT that drains 20 mana every 6 s (poison).
Discipline: If it's on you, you lose mana — it's a poison, not priest-removable.
Tank: Straight tank and spank.

Wolf Master Nandos · 25

Kill the four wolves (that pulls him). At 80% he calls a Bleak Worg or Lupine Horror — kill the summons first.

  • Call Bleak Worg, Call Lupine Horror.
Affliction: Corruption on every summon.
Tank: Thunder Clap, Taunt summons off the healer.

Archmage Arugal · 26

Kill the three worgen first. Line-of-sight Void Bolt; when he mind-controls someone, Taunt / CC them instead of killing them.

  • Void Bolt: 1399 shadow damage (not interruptible per Method). Arugal's Curse: 10 s mind control (not dispellable per WT). Shadow Port: teleports. Thundershock: AoE, 5 s stun.
Discipline: Shield whoever he Void Bolts. Don't waste heals on the mind-controlled player.
Affliction: Healthstone ready; don't DoT a mind-controlled party member.
Tank: He ports around — pick him up again fast. Shadow resistance gear helps.

Trash

  • Shadowfang Moonwalker — Anti-Magic Shield, 10 s full magic immunity.
  • Fel Steed — Fel Stomp: 3 s stun around it, more physical damage taken.
  • Haunted Servitor — Haunting Spirits curse (periodic ghosts).
  • Wailing Guardsman — Screams of the Past: 5 s silence in melee range. Kill first.
  • Tormented Officer — Forsaken Skills curse (a random skill −100 for 5 min).
  • Lupine Horror — Summon Lupine Delusions. Kill first.
  • Son of Arugal — Arugal's Gift: shadow DoT.
