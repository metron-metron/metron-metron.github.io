Ruins of Lordaeron

TL;DR

  • New Forever dungeon in the ruins just outside Undercity — the home dungeon for our three Undead.
  • 6 bosses + a rare (Lordaeron Captain, Horde only). Two of them are wave events (Viktor, The Abandoned).
  • Spiders poison, ghouls spread disease, Stone Watchers go physical-immune.
  • The Baron's Knockout is the tank killer: wall at the back, full HP before every hit.
  • Final boss Rath'mael in the fenced graveyard: dodge or interrupt Flamestrike.

Discipline Priest

  • The Baron's Knockout takes about half the tank's health and stuns 6 s: keep the tank near full, Shield before it lands.
  • Ghoul Plague (30 min, slow + less damage): Cure Disease it at once.
  • Viktor and The Abandoned are wave events — pace mana, wand between waves.

Affliction Warlock

  • Stone Watchers turn physical-immune: they're yours (magic).
  • Bjork's Anti-Magic Shield = 3 s spell immunity: pause casts, then re-DoT.
  • Rath'mael: stun or interrupt Flamestrike, and step out of it.

Warrior Tank

  • The Baron: back to a wall, stay near full HP before engaging. If you're stunned, he goes for someone else.
  • Ghoul packs: kill the Ragged Ghoul first. Living Monstrosity and Flesh Golem: alone only.

Route

  1. Entrance: Ruins of Lordaeron, through the front gate; before the keep, look left, up the stairs (~72.4, 11.6). Die after you're inside, not before — you ghost in the courtyard otherwise.
  2. Down the stairs heading east. Skip the spider-web gate: jump through the window on the right.
  3. Kings Alley: small spider packs, then Witherfang patrolling near the entrance. She doesn't bring her patrol along if you pull her from range.
  4. South along the left wall; at three Ghostly Citizens turn right into the passage west to The Baron's arena (skeleton packs of 3 around him).
  5. Behind The Baron: jump on the broken pillar and over the low wall. Dismiss pets first.
  6. Slightly right to the fenced graveyard with a summoning circle (Rath'mael's area — you come back here last). Keep right.
  7. Burned-down house past the graveyard: click the fireplace (Method: chimney) for Viktor the Vile — 5 waves of undead, Viktor with wave 6. Method calls him skippable.
  8. Courtyard with a glowing-eyed statue (Market Street): click it for The Abandoned — 3 waves, then the boss.
  9. Out of The Abandoned's courtyard by the narrow stairs, far end, turn right, jump the big window into Bjork's area. Clear the ghouls near him first.
  10. Straight south to the graveyard: Rath'mael. Run ends here (loot Edward's body after).
  • Rare: Lordaeron Captain on the western streets, small chance, attackable by Horde only. /target Lordaeron Captain along the way.

Bosses

Witherfang

Pull her from range without her Broodlings; if they come, kill the Broodlings first (WT) or her first (Method).
Light fight; Renew the tank.
DoTs, Shadow Bolt; Corruption the Broodlings if they come.
Ranged pull (shoot/throw) to split her from the patrol.

The Baron

Clear the skeleton packs, tank with your back to a wall, keep the tank near full HP. Ranged spread out.
Shield the tank before each Knockout; top the tank up fast after. Watch who he hits during the stun.
DoTs from range; if he turns to you during the stun, Voidwalker Sacrifice/Shield and walk back to the tank.
Wall at your back. Shield Block and Demoralizing Shout; don't let the healer fall behind before you engage.

Viktor the Vile

Click the fireplace when ready; 5 waves of undead, Viktor with wave 6. Little time between waves — manage mana.
Wand and stand still between waves; save mana for wave 6.
Corruption on each add, no big casts on mobs that die anyway. Life Tap between waves only if the healer has mana.
Thunder Clap + Demoralizing Shout per wave; Cleave (20) spare rage.

The Abandoned

Click the statue; 3 waves (Method says the boss comes with wave 4), then The Abandoned. Interrupt or stun his Life Drain.
Life Drain hits the tank: keep Renew up; Shield helps.
Range, so Frost Nova can't root you.
Shield Bash the Life Drain. Melee ranged-dps out before he Novas.

Bjork

Clear the ghouls in his area first; then tank and spank, casters pause during his shield.
Easy; Renew.
When the shell is up, stop casting, then re-apply DoTs.
Pull him away from ghoul packs.

Rath'mael

Interrupt or stun Flamestrike; if it lands, move out, then the tank moves him off the fire.
Stay out of melee range; heal the tank through any Flamestrike tick.
DoT from range. If the circle appears under you, move.
Shield Bash Flamestrike. If it lands, drag him out once the cast ends.

Lordaeron Captain (rare) · rare

Tank and spank. Hostile to Horde only; may not be up.
Pull him alone.
