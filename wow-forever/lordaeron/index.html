Ruins of Lordaeron

Run view ⤢

TL;DR

  • New Forever dungeon in the ruins just outside Undercity — the home dungeon for our three Undead.
  • 6 bosses + a rare (Lordaeron Captain, Horde only). Two of them are wave events (Viktor, The Abandoned).
  • Spiders poison, ghouls spread disease, Stone Watchers go physical-immune.
  • The Baron's Knockout is the tank killer: wall at the back, full HP before every hit.
  • Final boss Rath'mael in the fenced graveyard: dodge or interrupt Flamestrike.
Discipline Priest · Metrolyn
  • Dispel Magic (18) on Skeletal Mage Frost Armor.
  • Cure Disease for Ghoul Plague every time.
  • Lots of waves: stay above 50% mana before clicking the fireplace or statue.
Affliction Warlock · Metronom
  • Stone Watchers and Bjork are where your damage matters most.
  • Soulstone Metrolyn (or the healer) before the wave events (18+).
Warrior Tank · Metronis
  • The Baron is the one fight that kills tanks here. Everything else is about not overpulling (fast spider patrols, Flesh Golem).
  • Victory Rush (20) between waves.

Route

  1. Entrance: Ruins of Lordaeron, through the front gate; before the keep, look left, up the stairs (~72.4, 11.6). Die after you're inside, not before — you ghost in the courtyard otherwise.
  2. Down the stairs heading east. Skip the spider-web gate: jump through the window on the right.
  3. Kings Alley: small spider packs, then Witherfang patrolling near the entrance. She doesn't bring her patrol along if you pull her from range.
  4. South along the left wall; at three Ghostly Citizens turn right into the passage west to The Baron's arena (skeleton packs of 3 around him).
  5. Behind The Baron: jump on the broken pillar and over the low wall. Dismiss pets first.
  6. Slightly right to the fenced graveyard with a summoning circle (Rath'mael's area — you come back here last). Keep right.
  7. Burned-down house past the graveyard: click the fireplace (Method: chimney) for Viktor the Vile — 5 waves of undead, Viktor with wave 6. Method calls him skippable.
  8. Courtyard with a glowing-eyed statue (Market Street): click it for The Abandoned — 3 waves, then the boss.
  9. Out of The Abandoned's courtyard by the narrow stairs, far end, turn right, jump the big window into Bjork's area. Clear the ghouls near him first.
  10. Straight south to the graveyard: Rath'mael. Run ends here (loot Edward's body after).
  • Rare: Lordaeron Captain on the western streets, small chance, attackable by Horde only. /target Lordaeron Captain along the way.

Bosses

Witherfang · 17

Pull her from range without her Broodlings; if they come, kill the Broodlings first (WT) or her first (Method).

  • Leech Poison on the tank: small drain, cleansable, not urgent.
Discipline: Light fight; Renew the tank.
Affliction: DoTs, Shadow Bolt; Corruption the Broodlings if they come.
Tank: Ranged pull (shoot/throw) to split her from the patrol.

The Baron · 17

Clear the skeleton packs, tank with your back to a wall, keep the tank near full HP. Ranged spread out.

  • Knockout: a huge hit (about half the tank's health per Method) with knockback and a 6 s stun. Warcraft Tavern also mentions a melee-range AoE.
  • While the tank is stunned he picks someone else: that player kites or takes a hit or two.
Discipline: Shield the tank before each Knockout; top the tank up fast after. Watch who he hits during the stun.
Affliction: DoTs from range; if he turns to you during the stun, Voidwalker Sacrifice/Shield and walk back to the tank.
Tank: Wall at your back. Shield Block and Demoralizing Shout; don't let the healer fall behind before you engage.

Viktor the Vile · 19

Click the fireplace when ready; 5 waves of undead, Viktor with wave 6. Little time between waves — manage mana.

  • Leech Poison like Witherfang; otherwise tank and spank.
Discipline: Wand and stand still between waves; save mana for wave 6.
Affliction: Corruption on each add, no big casts on mobs that die anyway. Life Tap between waves only if the healer has mana.
Tank: Thunder Clap + Demoralizing Shout per wave; Cleave (20) spare rage.

The Abandoned · 18

Click the statue; 3 waves (Method says the boss comes with wave 4), then The Abandoned. Interrupt or stun his Life Drain.

  • Frost Nova: frost damage + 8 s root around him. Life Drain: drains 206 health from the tank. Frost Armor (not dispellable per WT); Chilled on melee attackers.
Discipline: Life Drain hits the tank: keep Renew up; Shield helps.
Affliction: Range, so Frost Nova can't root you.
Tank: Shield Bash the Life Drain. Melee ranged-dps out before he Novas.

Bjork · 19

Clear the ghouls in his area first; then tank and spank, casters pause during his shield.

  • Anti-Magic Shield: 3 s magic immunity.
Discipline: Easy; Renew.
Affliction: When the shell is up, stop casting, then re-apply DoTs.
Tank: Pull him away from ghoul packs.

Rath'mael · 20

Interrupt or stun Flamestrike; if it lands, move out, then the tank moves him off the fire.

  • Flamestrike: fire on an area — under himself (Method) or on the tank (Warcraft Tavern).
Discipline: Stay out of melee range; heal the tank through any Flamestrike tick.
Affliction: DoT from range. If the circle appears under you, move.
Tank: Shield Bash Flamestrike. If it lands, drag him out once the cast ends.

Lordaeron Captain (rare) · 19 · rare

Tank and spank. Hostile to Horde only; may not be up.

Tank: Pull him alone.

Trash

  • Spiders (Kings Alley: Broodwidows/Deep Widows, Venom Lurkers, Broodlings) — poison, fast patrols. Pull back to avoid overpulls.
  • Skeletal Soldier / Skeletal Mage — packs of 3 near The Baron. Mages cast Frost Armor: dispel it (Dispel Magic, 18) to speed the kill.
  • Banshees (Kings Alley past Witherfang) — curse that lowers hit chance.
  • Ghoul / Ragged Ghoul — tight packs of 3–4; kill the Ragged Ghoul first. Their hits can give Ghoul Plague (30 min disease: slow, less damage) — cure at once.
  • Flesh Golem — hard hits with knockback, patrols Lordamere Overlook. Don't get knocked into other packs.
  • Living Monstrosity — hits hard. Kill first, never with other trash.
  • Stone Watcher — gargoyles on upper floors that look like statues. Stone Slumber: physical immunity + regeneration. Use magic.
