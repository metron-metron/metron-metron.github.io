Hall of Thanes

Run view ⤢

TL;DR

  • New Forever dungeon under the High Seat of Ironforge — Alliance city. For Horde it's a long trek (Undercity → Silverpine → Hillsbrad → Arathi → Wetlands → Loch Modan → Dun Morogh) and no confirmed Horde quests. Only worth it in an Alliance-friendly group plan.
  • Ghosts first (easy), then Dark Iron dwarves (dangerous: casters and engineers).
  • Shoot the Seaforium kegs on the route to blow up dwarf packs.
  • Interrupt Dark Iron Summoner Summon Fiery Assistant and kill it first.
  • Final boss Durgen: single-target 8 s Fear and Rend on the tank, plus golem adds.
Discipline Priest · Metrolyn
  • Mana is fine on the ghosts; the dwarf half is where Shield + Renew matter.
  • Cure Disease (14) for Infected Wounds.
Affliction Warlock · Metronom
  • Keg first, then DoTs: one keg shot softens a whole pack.
  • Fear is risky in the narrow halls; skip it.
Warrior Tank · Metronis
  • LoS-pull every Summoner pack. Mark: Summoner → Engineer → Looter.
  • Golems never with another pack.

Route

  1. Entrance: inside the High Seat in Ironforge, spider-web passage on the left (~43.5, 52.0), down to the bridge. Lava below — a fall is fatal.
  2. Ghost halls: Tormented Souls and Enraged Apparitions. Turn into the west corridor.
  3. A Ghostly Attendant in the next chamber (quest NPC).
  4. Anvilmar's Rest: big tomb in the centre. Faldrim Anvilmar patrols among the ghosts — clear the ghosts first so he comes alone.
  5. Leave by the south-east corridor. From here: Dark Iron dwarves. Seaforium Blasting Powder kegs stand next to packs — shoot them from range.
  6. Natural cavern: Magmatus with a Dark Iron Summoner.
  7. Back in the hall: a pack of 4 dwarves with 2 kegs — shoot both kegs together before engaging.
  8. Plunder patrols the hallway. Clear the trash, then pull him alone.
  9. Bridge guarded by Lesser Stone Golems to the Reliquary of Kings.
  10. Final round room with vault doors: Durgen Dirgehammer with Dark Iron Looters and golems. Clear the room before the pull.
  11. Run ends at Durgen; walk out past the Ghostly Attendant.

Bosses

Faldrim Anvilmar · 16

Clear the ghosts around the tomb, then pull him alone. Tank and spank.

  • Mind Blast (interruptible, not required) and Anvilmar's Curse: −20% attack and cast speed for 10 min (also immunity to poison and bleeds). Dispel it if someone can; not mandatory.
Discipline: Curse isn't priest-removable. Renew + Lesser Heal is enough.
Affliction: If he curses you, your casts are 20% slower for 10 min — nothing to do but live with it.
Tank: Shield Bash a Mind Blast when convenient; the fight is easy.

Magmatus · 16

Kill the Dark Iron Summoner first while the tank drags Magmatus away from it; ranged stay out of Fire Nova range.

  • Fire Nova: instant fire around him. Combust: fire DoT on a random player.
Discipline: Watch everyone in melee and the Combust target, not just the tank.
Affliction: Summoner first (Corruption, Agony, Shadow Bolt), then Magmatus from max range.
Tank: Pick up Magmatus and turn him away from the Summoner; Shield Bash the Summoner's summon cast if you can reach it.

Plunder · 16

Back to a wall or corner, away from other trash; melee stay out of his charge.

  • Knock Away: knockback. Method also says he charges random melee players.
Discipline: Range from him; heal the tank after each knockback.
Affliction: Easy DoT fight from range.
Tank: Wall at your back so the knockback can't throw you into the next pack.

Durgen Dirgehammer · 16

Clear the room. On the pull: Durgen + 2 Lesser Stone Golems + Looters. Burn Durgen (Method) or the golems (Warcraft Tavern) — default: golems first if the tank is taking too much damage.

  • Fear: one target, up to 8 s. Rend: tank bleed (44 every 3 s per Method).
Discipline: Will of the Forsaken if feared. Big heals on the tank while golems are up.
Affliction: DoTs on Durgen and both golems; Fear none of them (they'd run into the vault room).
Tank: Will of the Forsaken breaks his Fear. Shield Block, Demoralizing Shout, Thunder Clap; Taunt a golem off the healer.

Trash

  • Dark Iron Summoner — heavy Fireball and Summon Fiery Assistant (interrupt). Pull out of line of sight; kill first.
  • Dark Iron Engineer — throws bombs at the tank's spot. The bomb lands where the cast started: step out.
  • Dark Iron Looter — poisoned blades, hits hard.
  • Bloodhound Runt — Infected Wounds disease (5 min, more physical damage taken). Cure Disease.
  • Lesser Stone Golem — hits hard and fast, faster than you run. Never pull with other packs.
  • Tormented Soul / Enraged Apparition — ghosts, nothing to note.
