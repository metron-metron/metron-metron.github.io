Hall of Thanes

TL;DR

  • New Forever dungeon under the High Seat of Ironforge — Alliance city. For Horde it's a long trek (Undercity → Silverpine → Hillsbrad → Arathi → Wetlands → Loch Modan → Dun Morogh) and no confirmed Horde quests. Only worth it in an Alliance-friendly group plan.
  • Ghosts first (easy), then Dark Iron dwarves (dangerous: casters and engineers).
  • Shoot the Seaforium kegs on the route to blow up dwarf packs.
  • Interrupt Dark Iron Summoner Summon Fiery Assistant and kill it first.
  • Final boss Durgen: single-target 8 s Fear and Rend on the tank, plus golem adds.

Discipline Priest

  • Durgen's Fear hits one target for 8 s — you're Undead: Will of the Forsaken breaks it.
  • Magmatus: Fire Nova and Combust hit melee and random players, not just the tank. Renew the melee.
  • Bloodhound Runt disease (Infected Wounds) — Cure Disease (14) the tank.

Affliction Warlock

  • Kill the Dark Iron Summoner before Magmatus; interruptless, so DoT it and Shadow Bolt.
  • Shoot Seaforium kegs from range to blow up packs (wand works).

Warrior Tank

  • Plunder: back to a wall so Knock Away doesn't throw you into other packs.
  • Durgen: two Lesser Stone Golems + Looters; Demoralizing Shout + Thunder Clap, Shield Block on cooldown.

Route

  1. Entrance: inside the High Seat in Ironforge, spider-web passage on the left (~43.5, 52.0), down to the bridge. Lava below — a fall is fatal.
  2. Ghost halls: Tormented Souls and Enraged Apparitions. Turn into the west corridor.
  3. A Ghostly Attendant in the next chamber (quest NPC).
  4. Anvilmar's Rest: big tomb in the centre. Faldrim Anvilmar patrols among the ghosts — clear the ghosts first so he comes alone.
  5. Leave by the south-east corridor. From here: Dark Iron dwarves. Seaforium Blasting Powder kegs stand next to packs — shoot them from range.
  6. Natural cavern: Magmatus with a Dark Iron Summoner.
  7. Back in the hall: a pack of 4 dwarves with 2 kegs — shoot both kegs together before engaging.
  8. Plunder patrols the hallway. Clear the trash, then pull him alone.
  9. Bridge guarded by Lesser Stone Golems to the Reliquary of Kings.
  10. Final round room with vault doors: Durgen Dirgehammer with Dark Iron Looters and golems. Clear the room before the pull.
  11. Run ends at Durgen; walk out past the Ghostly Attendant.

Bosses

Faldrim Anvilmar

Clear the ghosts around the tomb, then pull him alone. Tank and spank.
Curse isn't priest-removable. Renew + Lesser Heal is enough.
If he curses you, your casts are 20% slower for 10 min — nothing to do but live with it.
Shield Bash a Mind Blast when convenient; the fight is easy.

Magmatus

Kill the Dark Iron Summoner first while the tank drags Magmatus away from it; ranged stay out of Fire Nova range.
Watch everyone in melee and the Combust target, not just the tank.
Summoner first (Corruption, Agony, Shadow Bolt), then Magmatus from max range.
Pick up Magmatus and turn him away from the Summoner; Shield Bash the Summoner's summon cast if you can reach it.

Plunder

Back to a wall or corner, away from other trash; melee stay out of his charge.
Range from him; heal the tank after each knockback.
Easy DoT fight from range.
Wall at your back so the knockback can't throw you into the next pack.

Durgen Dirgehammer

Clear the room. On the pull: Durgen + 2 Lesser Stone Golems + Looters. Burn Durgen (Method) or the golems (Warcraft Tavern) — default: golems first if the tank is taking too much damage.
Will of the Forsaken if feared. Big heals on the tank while golems are up.
DoTs on Durgen and both golems; Fear none of them (they'd run into the vault room).
Will of the Forsaken breaks his Fear. Shield Block, Demoralizing Shout, Thunder Clap; Taunt a golem off the healer.
