Ragefire Chasm

TL;DR

  • Short one-map lava cave under Orgrimmar (Cleft of Shadow): 4 bosses, all level 16.
  • Danger is the trash, not the bosses: pull casters out of line of sight, don't pull two Enforcers at once.
  • Interrupt Ragefire Shaman Healing Wave.
  • Taragaman: stay off the platform edge — Uppercut knocks back into lava.
  • Jergosh and Bazzalan each bring two adds; kill the boss's adds or the boss first — see the boss notes.

Discipline Priest

  • Tank damage is low; the danger is Searing Blade packs pulled together. Power Word: Shield the tank on big pulls, Renew, then Lesser Heal / Heal.
  • Jergosh's Curse of Weakness and Bazzalan's Poison can't be removed by a priest — heal through.

Affliction Warlock

  • DoT the pack, never Fear: packs stand close and a runner pulls the next one.
  • Imp for damage; Voidwalker only if the tank struggles to hold packs.

Warrior Tank

  • LoS-pull every caster pack (Shaman, Warlock) round a corner.
  • Two Searing Blade Enforcers together can chain-stun you with Shield Slam — pull them apart.
  • Taragaman: tank him in the middle of the platform, back away from the lava (Uppercut).

Route

  1. Enter in the Cleft of Shadow (Orgrimmar, ~52.8, 49.6). Ramp down to a split with a path on each side.
  2. Take the right-hand branch first: troggs and Ragefire Shamans, then Oggleflint in the small chamber at its end.
  3. Back to the split, up the parallel ramp into a chamber, then the larger cavern with crossing paths (Molten Elementals, Searing Blade cultists).
  4. Taragaman the Hungerer on the middle platform by the lava. Clear patrols near him first; they join the fight otherwise.
  5. Continue to the Searing Blade area and clear the guards around Jergosh the Invoker.
  6. Last chamber: lower level, with an upper level running behind it. Bazzalan is on the upper level, at the very end.
  7. Run ends at Bazzalan. No doors, events or shortcuts.

Bosses

Oggleflint

Face him away from the group; mark and kill the troggs with him first.
Easy fight; Renew on the tank, top off melee hit by Cleave.
Corruption + Agony on the adds, then the boss. Stand at range, out of the Cleave arc.
Turn him away, Demoralizing Shout, Thunder Clap for the adds, Sunder the boss.

Taragaman the Hungerer

Tank in the middle of the platform with your back away from lava; melee get out of Fire Nova.
Fire Nova hits everyone in melee; Renew the melee and keep the tank above half for the Uppercut.
Full DoTs and Shadow Bolt from range; nothing to move for.
Position first: stand so the knockback throws you toward the middle, not the lava.

Jergosh the Invoker

Clear the guards first. Kill the two adds or Jergosh first (sources disagree) — default: CC/kill one add, burn Jergosh, then the other.
Mana fight. Use the wand between casts, don't overheal Immolate ticks; Shield the tank at the pull.
DoTs on all three; Drain Life if you take damage. Life Tap only when the healer is fine.
Shield Bash the Immolate. Thunder Clap + Demoralizing Shout to hold all three.

Bazzalan

Burn Bazzalan quickly, his two adds after (Method); Warcraft Tavern says adds first — pick what the group can CC.
Heaviest tank damage in the dungeon: Shield at pull, Renew, then Heal.
All DoTs on Bazzalan first; Corruption the adds if you have spare mana.
Shield Block (16+) on cooldown, Demoralizing Shout. Approach him carefully — the upper level is easy to overpull.
