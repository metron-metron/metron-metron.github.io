Ragefire Chasm

Run view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Short one-map lava cave under Orgrimmar (Cleft of Shadow): 4 bosses, all level 16.
  • Danger is the trash, not the bosses: pull casters out of line of sight, don't pull two Enforcers at once.
  • Interrupt Ragefire Shaman Healing Wave.
  • Taragaman: stay off the platform edge — Uppercut knocks back into lava.
  • Jergosh and Bazzalan each bring two adds; kill the boss's adds or the boss first — see the boss notes.
Discipline Priest · Metrolyn
  • Low-level dungeon: Lesser Heal, Renew and Power Word: Shield carry it; Heal from 16.
  • No dispel needed here: Curse of Weakness, Curse of Agony and Poison aren't priest-removable.
  • Psychic Scream (14) only in an emergency — fleeing mobs pull more packs in these tight caves.
Affliction Warlock · Metronom
  • No Fear here: packs are tight and runners bring friends.
  • Curse of Agony / Corruption spread on packs; Shadow Bolt the kill target.
  • Healthstone before the run (10+). Soul Shard from the last boss with Drain Soul.
Warrior Tank · Metronis
  • Line-of-sight pulls for every caster pack.
  • Mark kill order: Shaman (heals) → Warlock → Cultist → the rest.
  • Revenge (14) and Demoralizing Shout (14) arrive inside the level range — train them before the run.

Route

  1. Enter in the Cleft of Shadow (Orgrimmar, ~52.8, 49.6). Ramp down to a split with a path on each side.
  2. Take the right-hand branch first: troggs and Ragefire Shamans, then Oggleflint in the small chamber at its end.
  3. Back to the split, up the parallel ramp into a chamber, then the larger cavern with crossing paths (Molten Elementals, Searing Blade cultists).
  4. Taragaman the Hungerer on the middle platform by the lava. Clear patrols near him first; they join the fight otherwise.
  5. Continue to the Searing Blade area and clear the guards around Jergosh the Invoker.
  6. Last chamber: lower level, with an upper level running behind it. Bazzalan is on the upper level, at the very end.
  7. Run ends at Bazzalan. No doors, events or shortcuts.

Bosses

Oggleflint · 16

Face him away from the group; mark and kill the troggs with him first.

  • Cleave is his only ability. Comes with trogg adds (two per Warcraft Tavern, "several" per Method) — pull the loose troggs in the room before him.
Discipline: Easy fight; Renew on the tank, top off melee hit by Cleave.
Affliction: Corruption + Agony on the adds, then the boss. Stand at range, out of the Cleave arc.
Tank: Turn him away, Demoralizing Shout, Thunder Clap for the adds, Sunder the boss.

Taragaman the Hungerer · 16

Tank in the middle of the platform with your back away from lava; melee get out of Fire Nova.

  • Fire Nova: instant fire damage around him. Uppercut: hits the tank and knocks them back and up — near the edge that's a lava swim.
  • No adds of his own, but patrols on the platform join if not cleared.
Discipline: Fire Nova hits everyone in melee; Renew the melee and keep the tank above half for the Uppercut.
Affliction: Full DoTs and Shadow Bolt from range; nothing to move for.
Tank: Position first: stand so the knockback throws you toward the middle, not the lava.

Jergosh the Invoker · 16

Clear the guards first. Kill the two adds or Jergosh first (sources disagree) — default: CC/kill one add, burn Jergosh, then the other.

  • Immolate on his target (fire DoT, interruptible per Warcraft Tavern) and Curse of Weakness.
  • A caster fight: healer mana runs low here.
Discipline: Mana fight. Use the wand between casts, don't overheal Immolate ticks; Shield the tank at the pull.
Affliction: DoTs on all three; Drain Life if you take damage. Life Tap only when the healer is fine.
Tank: Shield Bash the Immolate. Thunder Clap + Demoralizing Shout to hold all three.

Bazzalan · 16

Burn Bazzalan quickly, his two adds after (Method); Warcraft Tavern says adds first — pick what the group can CC.

  • Poison: nature DoT on the tank (curable by a poison cleanse, not by a priest). Sinister Strike: instant hit.
  • Hurts the tank more than the other three bosses.
Discipline: Heaviest tank damage in the dungeon: Shield at pull, Renew, then Heal.
Affliction: All DoTs on Bazzalan first; Corruption the adds if you have spare mana.
Tank: Shield Block (16+) on cooldown, Demoralizing Shout. Approach him carefully — the upper level is easy to overpull.

Trash

  • Ragefire Shaman — Lightning Bolt from range; interrupt Healing Wave. Break line of sight to bring them to you.
  • Searing Blade Warlock — spams Shadow Bolt on the tank, comes with a Voidwalker Minion. LoS-pull.
  • Searing Blade Enforcer — Shield Slam stuns the tank for 2 s; two Enforcers can chain-stun. Don't overpull.
  • Searing Blade Cultist — Curse of Agony on the tank. Kill quickly.
  • Molten Elemental — Fire Shield damages melee attackers. Casters kill these.
  • Ragefire Trogg — melee, nothing to note.
