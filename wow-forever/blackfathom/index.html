Ashenvale · level 24–32 · unchecked

Blackfathom Deeps

Run view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Zoram Strand, north-west Ashenvale (flight path Zoram'gar). Lots of swimming.
  • Naga, satyr and Twilight's Hammer trash. Kill Twilight Shadowmages first (mind control) and interrupt Twilight Acolyte Heal and Fallenroot Hellcaller Mana Burn.
  • Bosses: Ghamoo-ra, Lady Sarevess, Gelihast (optional), Old Serra'kis (underwater, optional), Twilight Lord Kelris, Aku'mai. Horde quest bosses: Lorgus Jett, Baron Aquanis.
  • Before Aku'mai: light 4 braziers one at a time — each spawns a wave.
  • Aku'mai: move out of Poison Cloud; save stuns for Frenzied Rage.
Discipline Priest · Metrolyn
  • Mana Burn and mind control are the dangers; stay out of Hellcaller range until it's dead.
  • Kelris sleeps, he doesn't fear: Fear Ward won't help there; Will of the Forsaken does.
Affliction Warlock · Metronom
  • Felhunter (30) Devour Magic is useful on Sleep and on Acolytes' Renew.
Warrior Tank · Metronis
  • Disarmed often: Sunder, Revenge, Shield Bash still work.

Route

  1. Entrance in the sunken ruins on the Zoram Strand coast (~14.5, 14.6). Swim the pool at the bottom, then a narrow winding cave with elite naga.
  2. Ghamoo-ra on his island. A chest with the Lorgalis Manuscript sits in a niche north of the island. An underwater passage south-west leads to Argent Guard Thaelrid.
  3. Lady Sarevess with two naga adds.
  4. Gelihast (optional) in the murloc room — pull the murlocs in ones and twos first. Click the stone behind him afterwards for Blessing of Blackfathom.
  5. Lorgus Jett (Horde quest target) — easy.
  6. Old Serra'kis (optional) underwater — watch your breath.
  7. Baron Aquanis (Horde quest): the Fathom Stone underwater in his room summons him.
  8. Twilight Lord Kelris guards the Twilight quarters.
  9. Past Kelris: the brazier room. Light the 4 braziers one at a time: bottom right (2 Aku'mai Servants per side), bottom left (3 Snapjaws per side), top right (10 Murkshallow Softshells per side), top left (4 Barbed Crustaceans per side).
  10. Aku'mai in the secret chamber. Run ends.

Bosses

Ghamoo-ra · 25

Tank faces him away; reposition after Trample. High armour — Sunder helps.

  • Trample: physical AoE around him. Can be stunned and feared (WT).
Discipline: Range from him; easy fight.
Affliction: Casters do the damage (armour).
Tank: Sunder Armor stacks first.

Lady Sarevess · 25

CC one naga add and kill the other, then her. Face her away and spread — Forked Lightning is a cone that chains.

  • Forked Lightning (interruptible), Frost Nova (near her, or when you interrupt per WT), Slow on a random target, Shoot.
Discipline: Stand away from the tank and from others.
Affliction: Fear or Succubus Seduce an add if the group asks.
Tank: Shield Bash Forked Lightning; Thunder Clap to hold the adds.

Gelihast · 26

Optional. Pull the murlocs first in ones and twos; then the boss.

  • Net: 10 s root (WT: lowers threat).
Tank: Netted: keep Sundering; Taunt if he turns.

Lorgus Jett · 26

Tank and spank (Horde quest kill).

  • Lightning Shield (damages attackers), Lightning Bolt.
Tank: Interrupt Lightning Bolt.

Old Serra'kis · 26

Optional, underwater: watch breath. No abilities per Method; WT says he heals himself.

Baron Aquanis · 28

Summoned from the Fathom Stone (Horde quest). Tank and spank.

  • Frostbolt, Frost Nova.
Tank: Shield Bash Frostbolts.

Twilight Lord Kelris · 27

Melee and healer spread; Will of the Forsaken out of Sleep. If the tank is slept, someone holds him.

  • Sleep: nearby players, 10 s. Mind Blast on the tank (heavy shadow, not interruptible per WT).
Discipline: Dispel Magic a slept tank (magic sleep).
Affliction: Voidwalker can Taunt-hold him if the tank sleeps.
Tank: WotF out of Sleep, then Taunt him back.

Aku'mai · 28

Clear the brazier waves. Move out of Poison Cloud (527 nature every 5 s for 45 s; interruptible per WT). Stun or slow during Frenzied Rage (+75% attack speed, 5 s).

Discipline: Big tank heals during Frenzied Rage; you can't cure the poison.
Affliction: Steady DoTs from range; out of the cloud.
Tank: Shield Block / Shield Wall for the frenzy. Move him out of the cloud if it's under the group.

Trash

  • Twilight Shadowmage — Dominate Mind (mind control). Kill first; LoS-pull.
  • Twilight Acolyte — Heal (interrupt/stun), Renew.
  • Fallenroot Hellcaller — Mana Burn, Shadow Bolt. Interrupt or stun.
  • Twilight Loreseeker — Slow, Enlarge.
  • Twilight Elementalist — Earth Shock, Flame Shock.
  • Blackfathom Sea Witch — Frost Nova.
  • Blackfathom Myrmidon / Murkshallow Snapclaw — disarm the tank (8 s), heavy physical.
  • Fallenroot Shadowstalker — stealthed; Curse of Weakness on the tank.
