Wailing Caverns

Run view ⤢

TL;DR

  • Barrens, Lushwater Oasis. Long dungeon (30–40 min): 4 Druid of the Fang bosses, Kresh, Skum, Verdan, then the Disciple of Naralex escort to Mutanus.
  • Interrupt Druids of the Fang: Healing Touch, Sleep (Druid's Slumber), Lightning Bolt.
  • Undead: Will of the Forsaken breaks Sleep, Fear and Naralex's Nightmare.
  • Poison and nature damage everywhere.
  • Escort only after the four Druid bosses are dead; Mutanus comes at the end.
Discipline Priest · Metrolyn
  • Sleep and fear are the threat to you — WotF has a cooldown, so don't spend it on a fear that ends anyway.
  • Dispel Magic (18) on a slept party member (magic sleep, worth trying).
Affliction Warlock · Metronom
  • Fear is fine in the open caverns, not in the narrow tunnels.
  • Curse of Recklessness (14) on fleeing Druids if they run.
Warrior Tank · Metronis
  • Kill order on Druid packs: the Druid (heals, sleeps) first.
  • You're the main interrupt: Shield Bash on cooldown.

Route

  1. Entrance: the skull-shaped cave north-west of Lushwater Oasis (~46, 36). The outdoor cave isn't the portal: go down to the big pool cavern; elites patrol the outer cave.
  2. Inside, the Disciple of Naralex waits at the start — don't start the escort yet.
  3. Central cavern with a stream: Lady Anacondra spawns at one of the ritual circles on the upper ledges (random).
  4. Left wing, winding path up: Lord Cobrahn in the Pit of Fangs.
  5. Back to the centre: Kresh patrols the stream.
  6. The larger wing: Lord Pythas, Skum (Winding Chasm), Lord Serpentis, then Verdan the Everliving by the waterfall. One gap needs a jump.
  7. After Verdan: jump into the pool behind him; it brings you back near the entrance.
  8. Talk to the Disciple of Naralex and escort him to the final room. Clear before you start; waves come during the event.
  9. Mutanus the Devourer rises from the pool once the Disciple reaches the ritual stone. Run ends.
  • Rare: Deviate Faerie Dragon, infrequent spawn with a couple of Druids.

Bosses

Lady Anacondra · 20

Pull carefully (Druids and raptors nearby); interrupt Healing Touch and Sleep.

  • Healing Touch, Sleep (can't hit the tank), Thorns Aura (damages melee attackers; can't be purged), Lightning Bolt.
Discipline: Will of the Forsaken out of Sleep; Renew on the tank before she casts.
Affliction: DoTs and Shadow Bolt — ranged is safe from Thorns.
Tank: Save Shield Bash for Healing Touch.

Lord Cobrahn · 20

Kill the three Deviate Pythons first. Interrupt Healing Touch; at about 50% he turns into a serpent and hits much harder.

  • Lightning Bolt, Healing Touch, Druid's Slumber (8 s sleep, can hit the healer), Cobrahn Serpent Form.
Discipline: HoT the tank before serpent form; Shield on the transform.
Affliction: Corruption on the pythons, then all DoTs on Cobrahn.
Tank: Shield Block and Demoralizing Shout once he's a serpent.

Kresh · 20

Tank and spank, facing away.

  • High armour, reduced physical damage taken. Shell Shield in the journal; Bite per Method.
Discipline: Easy.
Affliction: You're the damage — DoTs and Shadow Bolt.
Tank: Sunder stacks matter here; long fight.

Lord Pythas · 21

Interrupt Healing Touch and Sleep; melee mind the Thunderclap.

  • Thunderclap: nature AoE, slows movement and attacks. Healing Touch, Sleep. Adds: a Druid of the Fang and a Deviate Shambler (Method).
Discipline: Renew the melee after Thunderclap.
Affliction: DoTs on Pythas and the adds.
Tank: Shield Bash Healing Touch first, Sleep second.

Skum · 21

Face away from the group; spread so Chained Bolt can't jump.

  • Immune to Nature damage. Chained Bolt is interruptible.
Discipline: Stand away from other players.
Affliction: Shadow is fine. Keep your distance from the group.
Tank: Shield Bash Chained Bolt.

Lord Serpentis · 21

Interrupt Healing Touch and Sleep. Two Druid adds (Method) or none (WT).

  • Healing Touch (heals a lot), Sleep, Lightning Bolt.
Discipline: Will of the Forsaken out of Sleep.
Affliction: If adds: DoT them, then him.
Tank: Every Healing Touch must be interrupted.

Verdan the Everliving · 21

Healer and ranged at max range (10+ yd). Tank uses defensives; hits very hard.

  • Grasping Vines: everyone within 10 yd knocked down and rooted 10 s, not interruptible.
Discipline: Heal from max range nonstop; Shield the tank when Vines land.
Affliction: Stay out of 10 yd. Steady DoTs.
Tank: Shield Block, Demoralizing Shout. Save Shield Wall (28) if you have it.

Mutanus the Devourer · 22

During the escort, kill the add waves before the boss emerges. On Mutanus: interrupt Naralex's Nightmare, melee expect a stun after Thundercrack.

  • Thundercrack: nature AoE, 2.5 s stun around him. Terrify: 4 s fear on non-tanks. Naralex's Nightmare: sleep.
Discipline: Max range; Will of the Forsaken out of Terrify or Nightmare.
Affliction: Ranged; WotF if feared or slept.
Tank: Keep him away from the Disciple; defensive after each Thundercrack.

Deviate Faerie Dragon (rare) · 20 · rare

Kill its Druids of the Fang first, then the dragon.

Trash

  • Druid of the Fang — Lightning Bolt, Healing Touch, Druid's Slumber (sleeps anyone, incl. the healer), turns into a serpent near death (heavy hits). Interrupt Slumber/Healing Touch.
  • Deviate Viper — Localized Toxin on the tank (poison).
  • Deviate Shambler — Wild Regeneration heals; interrupt.
  • Deviate Dreadfang — Terrify (fear), Bite, Lightning Breath.
  • Deviate Ravager / Guardian — raptors, physical only.
