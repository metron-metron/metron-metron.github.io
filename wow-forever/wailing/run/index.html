Wailing Caverns

TL;DR

  • Barrens, Lushwater Oasis. Long dungeon (30–40 min): 4 Druid of the Fang bosses, Kresh, Skum, Verdan, then the Disciple of Naralex escort to Mutanus.
  • Interrupt Druids of the Fang: Healing Touch, Sleep (Druid's Slumber), Lightning Bolt.
  • Undead: Will of the Forsaken breaks Sleep, Fear and Naralex's Nightmare.
  • Poison and nature damage everywhere.
  • Escort only after the four Druid bosses are dead; Mutanus comes at the end.

Discipline Priest

  • Druids of the Fang sleep the healer: Will of the Forsaken breaks Sleep. Keep Renew on the tank so a sleep doesn't wipe.
  • Deviate Viper Localized Toxin is a poison — not priest-curable; heal through.
  • Mutanus: stand at max range, top off melee after Thundercrack.

Affliction Warlock

  • Skum is Nature-immune — nothing changes for Shadow.
  • Kresh has high armour: casters are the damage here.
  • Mutanus casts Naralex's Nightmare — Will of the Forsaken if it hits you.

Warrior Tank

  • Shield Bash the Druids' Healing Touch and Sleep; Lightning Bolt when free.
  • Verdan: Grasping Vines roots and knocks down everyone within 10 yd — use defensives, healer at range.

Route

  1. Entrance: the skull-shaped cave north-west of Lushwater Oasis (~46, 36). The outdoor cave isn't the portal: go down to the big pool cavern; elites patrol the outer cave.
  2. Inside, the Disciple of Naralex waits at the start — don't start the escort yet.
  3. Central cavern with a stream: Lady Anacondra spawns at one of the ritual circles on the upper ledges (random).
  4. Left wing, winding path up: Lord Cobrahn in the Pit of Fangs.
  5. Back to the centre: Kresh patrols the stream.
  6. The larger wing: Lord Pythas, Skum (Winding Chasm), Lord Serpentis, then Verdan the Everliving by the waterfall. One gap needs a jump.
  7. After Verdan: jump into the pool behind him; it brings you back near the entrance.
  8. Talk to the Disciple of Naralex and escort him to the final room. Clear before you start; waves come during the event.
  9. Mutanus the Devourer rises from the pool once the Disciple reaches the ritual stone. Run ends.
  • Rare: Deviate Faerie Dragon, infrequent spawn with a couple of Druids.

Bosses

Lady Anacondra

Pull carefully (Druids and raptors nearby); interrupt Healing Touch and Sleep.
Will of the Forsaken out of Sleep; Renew on the tank before she casts.
DoTs and Shadow Bolt — ranged is safe from Thorns.
Save Shield Bash for Healing Touch.

Lord Cobrahn

Kill the three Deviate Pythons first. Interrupt Healing Touch; at about 50% he turns into a serpent and hits much harder.
HoT the tank before serpent form; Shield on the transform.
Corruption on the pythons, then all DoTs on Cobrahn.
Shield Block and Demoralizing Shout once he's a serpent.

Kresh

Tank and spank, facing away.
Easy.
You're the damage — DoTs and Shadow Bolt.
Sunder stacks matter here; long fight.

Lord Pythas

Interrupt Healing Touch and Sleep; melee mind the Thunderclap.
Renew the melee after Thunderclap.
DoTs on Pythas and the adds.
Shield Bash Healing Touch first, Sleep second.

Skum

Face away from the group; spread so Chained Bolt can't jump.
Stand away from other players.
Shadow is fine. Keep your distance from the group.
Shield Bash Chained Bolt.

Lord Serpentis

Interrupt Healing Touch and Sleep. Two Druid adds (Method) or none (WT).
Will of the Forsaken out of Sleep.
If adds: DoT them, then him.
Every Healing Touch must be interrupted.

Verdan the Everliving

Healer and ranged at max range (10+ yd). Tank uses defensives; hits very hard.
Heal from max range nonstop; Shield the tank when Vines land.
Stay out of 10 yd. Steady DoTs.
Shield Block, Demoralizing Shout. Save Shield Wall (28) if you have it.

Mutanus the Devourer

During the escort, kill the add waves before the boss emerges. On Mutanus: interrupt Naralex's Nightmare, melee expect a stun after Thundercrack.
Max range; Will of the Forsaken out of Terrify or Nightmare.
Ranged; WotF if feared or slept.
Keep him away from the Disciple; defensive after each Thundercrack.

Deviate Faerie Dragon (rare) · rare

Kill its Druids of the Fang first, then the dragon.
