Findings

  1. Most net gain so far (+942k, +512 raid DPS over 31 min of trash), but the share of best held at 64%: the raid did more spell damage, so every curse was worth more and so was the best plan.
  2. The extra 12 curses went to "if_free" (11% → 44%) and "skip" mobs (3 → 8), not to the "curse" tier (49% both nights). Bonechewer packs show it most: Blade Furies cursed while Shield Disciples, Blood Prophets and Mutant War Hounds stayed uncursed (pulls 17 and 18, −99k against best). Possible cause, not proven: magenta and dark magenta are hard to tell apart on nameplates.
  3. Gauntlets are still the largest gap: Ashtongue run (pulls 10–11, −134k), Shadowmoon gauntlet (pulls 12–13, −82k).
  4. Skip curses: Coilskar Wrangler ×2, Shadowmoon War Hound ×2, Dragonmaw Wyrmcaller, Shadowmoon Soldier, Ardent Host, Illidari Battle-mage. Three Devoted Steward curses were worth 2k each (dying adds).
  5. Shadow Bolts before the first Seed are back at 32 (10 on one pull at 20:51). Seeds on cursed mobs doubled to 40 but still cost only ≈12k.
