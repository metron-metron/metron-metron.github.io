Black Temple only. Share = your damage ÷ the raid's (pets included; Teron's ghost abilities left out). Curse coverage = share of the fire, frost, arcane and shadow damage on the bosses (units that took ≥ 10% of it) that landed while Curse of the Elements was up. "Added" = what your curse added against no curse at all (+13%; +10% on a pull where you were Destruction). Pause between casts = the wait from one spell (or its global cooldown) ending to the next starting, while casting back to back; pauses of 1 s or more count as movement and show in Active instead. Lower is better. Moving = distance moved ÷ run speed (7 yd/s); without casting = moving time when no cast or global cooldown was running, i.e. when an instant could have gone out. Both lower is better. Supremus and Mother Shahraz are left out of the moving totals: his chase and her Fatal Attraction pick players at random and make them run.

Findings

  1. Clear improvement: +264k more net from the same number of pulls, the "curse" tier went from 1 in 4 to 1 in 2, and the Shadow Bolt openers are gone. The list plan would have given +979k this night; he got 80% of that.
  2. Small packs and single big mobs are solved: 14 of 33 pulls are within 2k of best.
  3. Over half the remaining gap (199k of 383k) is the two gauntlets: the Ashtongue run before Akama (pull 10, 335 s, gap 122k) and the Shadowmoon gauntlet (pull 13, 227 s, gap 77k). Curses come in bursts (Ashtongue run: one at 9 s, then nothing until 137 s) while new mobs keep arriving. In a gauntlet even the mobs then in "if_free" paid (Ashtongue Primalist 9–13k, now "curse"; Shadowmoon Riding Hound 8–11k, Shadowmoon Reaver 10–11k) because they live long: curse every new arrival with 150k+ HP there.
  4. Mixed Illidari / Shadowmoon packs: the curse went on the wrong mob twice — pull 22 (Boneslicer, 2k, while Heartseeker and Centurion were worth 9–11k) and pull 14 (Wrathbone Flayer, 14k, uncursed).
  5. Waste is small: 3 curses on skip mobs (Coilskar Wrangler ×2, Temple Acolyte), 2 on Council-entrance mobs that reset at once (pull 31). Seeds on cursed mobs cost ≈6k all night — no longer worth thinking about.
