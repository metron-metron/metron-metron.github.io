Findings

  1. Single big mobs and pairs (Sentinels, Fearbringers, Aqueous Lords, Mistresses, Behemoths) were already played right.
  2. Almost all of the missing value is in mixed packs of 4–12 mobs at 150–300k HP (Ashtongue run, Shadowmoon gauntlet, Bonechewer packs, Illidari packs before Council): one curse per pack, often on the mob that died first.
  3. Curses on mobs about to die (Heartseeker 1 s, Assassin 3 s, Steward 5 s) are worth less than the GCD.
  4. Shadow Bolts before the first Seed (3.6k each) are worth less than a curse on a big uncursed mob (about 6.6k at 13%, plus the 1 s a Shadow Bolt cast costs over a GCD).
